The **SSC CHSL (Combined Higher Secondary Level)** examination mandates a qualifying **Skill Test / Typing Test** as part of Tier-II. The test differs depending on whether you applied for **Lower Division Clerk (LDC) / Junior Secretariat Assistant (JSA)** or **Data Entry Operator (DEO)**.

---

## 1. Post-Wise Speed Requirements & Test Duration

| Post Applied | Medium | Duration | Key Depressions (KDPH) | Net Speed Required |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **LDC / JSA** | English | 10 Minutes | 10,500 KDPH | **35 WPM** |
| **LDC / JSA** | Hindi | 10 Minutes | 9,000 KDPH | **30 WPM** |
| **DEO (General)** | English | 15 Minutes | 8,000 KDPH | **27 WPM** |
| **DEO Grade 'A' (CAG/Ministries)** | English | 15 Minutes | 15,000 KDPH | **50 WPM** |

---

## 2. Category-Wise Permissible Error Percentage

| Category | Permissible Error Limit (LDC/JSA) | Permissible Error Limit (DEO) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **UR (General)** | **7%** | **5%** |
| **OBC / EWS** | **10%** | **7%** |
| **SC / ST** | **10%** | **7%** |
| **Ex-Servicemen (ESM)** | **10%** | **7%** |
| **PwD** | **10%** | **7%** |

---

## 3. How Words and Key Depressions are Calculated

In SSC examinations, **1 standard word = 5 key depressions (keystrokes)**, including spaces and punctuation marks.

$$\text{Words Typed} = \frac{\text{Total Key Depressions Typed}}{5}$$

$$\text{Gross Speed (WPM)} = \frac{\text{Words Typed}}{\text{Duration in Minutes}}$$

$$\text{Net Speed (WPM)} = \frac{\text{Words Typed} - (\text{Mistakes} \times \text{Penalty})}{\text{Duration in Minutes}}$$

## 4. Key Takeaways for Aspirants
* **Hindi Font**: SSC CHSL Hindi typing uses **Mangal font** (with Remington Gail or Inscript layout).
* **10-Minute Endurance**: Unlike 1-minute speed tests, maintaining high velocity over 10 continuous minutes without pausing is critical.
