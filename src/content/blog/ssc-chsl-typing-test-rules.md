## 1. Post-Wise Speed Requirements & Math

In SSC technical standards, **1 standard word = 5 key depressions (keystrokes)**, including spacebar hits and punctuation marks.

### 🔹 For Lower Division Clerk (LDC) & Junior Secretariat Assistant (JSA)
- **English Medium**: Required speed is **10,500 KDPH** (Key Depressions Per Hour). In a 10-minute test, this equals exactly:
  $$\frac{10500 \text{ KDPH} \times 10 \text{ mins}}{60 \text{ mins}} = \mathbf{1,750 \text{ Key Depressions}}$$
  $$\frac{1750 \text{ KD}}{5 \text{ keys/word} \times 10 \text{ mins}} = \mathbf{35 \text{ Words Per Minute (WPM)}}$$
- **Hindi Medium**: Required speed is **9,000 KDPH**, which equals **1,500 key depressions in 10 minutes** ($\mathbf{30 \text{ WPM}}$).

### 🔹 For Data Entry Operator (DEO) Posts
- **DEO Grade 'A' (Offices under CAG, Ministry of Consumer Affairs, etc.)**: Required speed is **15,000 KDPH**, translating to **3,750 key depressions in 15 minutes** ($\mathbf{50 \text{ WPM}}$).
- **Other Departments DEO**: 8,000 KDPH, translating to **2,000 key depressions in 15 minutes** ($\mathbf{27 \text{ WPM}}$).

## 3. Official SSC Mistake Evaluation Algorithm

The evaluation software analyzes your transcript against the master passage using character-level string comparison:

### 🔴 Full Mistakes (100% Penalty / 1.0 Count)
1. **Omission of Word**: Skipping any word, phrase, or line from the original passage.
2. **Substitution of Word**: Replacing an original word with an incorrect word (e.g. typing *computer* instead of *commission*).
3. **Addition / Insertion**: Typing words that do not exist in the master text.
4. **Spelling Mistake**: Every wrong character, missing character, or repeated letter in a word.
5. **Incomplete Word**: Leaving a word half-typed when the 10-minute timer expires.

### 🟡 Half Mistakes (50% Penalty / 0.5 Count)
1. **Spacing Errors**: Not leaving space between two words (e.g., `thecourt` instead of `the court`) or giving double spaces between words.
2. **Punctuation Discrepancies**: Missing or incorrect commas, full stops, question marks, or apostrophes.
3. **Capitalization Errors**: Typing lowercase where capital letters were required (e.g., `india` instead of `India`, or vice versa).
4. **Transposition of Characters**: Swapping adjacent letters within an otherwise correct word (e.g., `recieve` instead of `receive`).

## 4. Official SSC Error Percentage Formula

The commission computes your qualifying status with this mathematical equation:

$$\text{Total Mistakes} = \text{Full Mistakes} + \left( \frac{\text{Half Mistakes}}{2} \right)$$

$$\text{Percentage Error (\%)} = \left( \frac{\text{Total Mistakes} \times 100}{\text{Total Words in Master Passage}} \right)$$

### 📊 Real Example Calculation:
* Master passage length: **350 words** (approx 1750 key depressions).
* Full mistakes made: **14**
* Half mistakes made: **16**
* Step 1: $\text{Total Mistakes} = 14 + (16 / 2) = 14 + 8 = \mathbf{22}$
* Step 2: $\text{Error Percentage} = \frac{22 \times 100}{350} = \mathbf{6.28\%}$
* **Verdict**: 
  - **UR / General (7% limit)**: $\mathbf{6.28\% \le 7\%}$ $\rightarrow$ **QUALIFIED (Pass)** ✅
  - If total mistakes had been 26, Error % would be $7.42\%$ $\rightarrow$ **DISQUALIFIED (Fail)** ❌

## 5. Ground Reality: TCS iON Exam Software Behavior

Candidates who practice solely on casual online typing games frequently fail on exam day due to unexpected software constraints:

1. **No Word Highlighting**: Unlike third-party apps, the official TCS iON testing engine does **not** highlight the active word in yellow or change errors to red. You must track your cursor position manually on screen.
2. **No Auto-Scrolling**: The master passage box does not scroll automatically. As you type past the visible screen lines, you must manually grab the mouse or hit the down-arrow key to reveal the next paragraph.
3. **Backspace Functionality**: The Backspace key and arrow keys are **fully enabled**. You can backspace across multiple words to correct a mistake, but doing so burns precious seconds.
4. **The 5-Minute Mock Trial Run**: Before the main 10-minute test begins, the system conducts a mandatory **5-minute demo typing test**. Use this demo session to physically verify every critical key:
   - Spacebar responsiveness (does it double-trigger or fail to register?).
   - Enter key, Backspace key, and both Shift keys (Left & Right).
   - If any key sticks or requires heavy force, **raise your hand immediately to replace your keyboard before the real exam timer starts**.
