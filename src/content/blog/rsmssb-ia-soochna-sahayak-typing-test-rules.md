The **Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSSB / RSMSSB, Jaipur)** conducts the state-level recruitment for **Informatics Assistant (सूचना सहायक - IA)** across government secretariats, revenue departments, and DOIT&C Rajasthan.

Candidates who clear the Phase-I Written Examination (called at **3 times the number of vacancies**) must appear for the mandatory **Phase-II Typing Test**.

## 1. Why Many Candidates Fail a "Low" 15 WPM Speed Requirement

On paper, **15 WPM** sounds extremely easy. However, in previous RSMSSB IA examinations, nearly **25% to 30% of candidates failed the typing test** because of three critical mistakes:
1. **Neglecting Hindi Typing**: Many IT/Computer graduates (BCA, B.Tech, MCA) are proficient in English but have never practiced Hindi typing. Scoring 40 WPM in English and 14.8 WPM in Hindi results in instant disqualification!
2. **Keyboard Freezing & Anxiety**: The 15-minute duration requires sustained concentration.
3. **Alt Codes Errors in DevLys/KrutiDev**: Inability to type words like *कृषि, द्वितीय, क्रमांक, दृष्टि* leads to accumulated errors.

---

## 2. Speed Calculation & Formula

`Gross Words = (Total Key Depressions Typed / 5)`

`Net Speed (WPM) = (Gross Words - Mistakes Penalty / Time (15 Minutes))`

* Minimum threshold: You must successfully complete at least **225 words with high accuracy** in each 15-minute slot.
