The **West Bengal Public Service Commission (WBPSC Clerkship)**, **Odisha Staff Selection Commission (OSSC / OSSSC)**, and **Jammu & Kashmir Services Selection Board (JKSSB)** conduct state-level examinations for **Junior Assistant, Junior Clerk, and Typist-Copyist** posts.

## 1. WBPSC Clerkship Speed & Evaluation Guidelines

* **English Typing Benchmark**: Candidates must type at least **20 Words Per Minute (WPM)** on standard computer keyboards.
* **Bengali Typing Benchmark**: Candidates choosing Bengali must type at least **10 Words Per Minute** using Avro or standard Bengali Inscript Unicode layout.
* **Elementary Computer Test**: Evaluates basic word processing, table generation, and spreadsheet formatting.

---

## 2. JKSSB Junior Assistant 35 WPM Benchmark

In JKSSB, the Type Test is conducted as **Stage-I**:
* Only candidates who clear the 35 WPM (90% accuracy) threshold are admitted to the written examination.
* Test is conducted on dedicated exam software with live speed calculation.
