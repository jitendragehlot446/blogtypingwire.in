## 1. The SBI & IBPS Descriptive Test: The Hidden Typing Requirement

While IBPS and SBI PO do not conduct a separate "Speed Typing Test", candidates face a hidden typing challenge in the **Tier-II Mains Examination**:

* **The Descriptive Paper**: Candidates must type an **Essay (approx. 250 words)** and a **Letter (approx. 150 words)** directly onto a computer terminal within **30 minutes** (Total 400 words).
* **Speed Reality**: While 400 words in 30 minutes theoretically equals only **13.3 WPM**, candidates must also think, formulate arguments, organize paragraphs, and correct grammatical typos.
* **Typing Bottleneck**: Candidates with slow hunt-and-peck typing speeds (under 20 WPM) run out of time and fail to complete their essay, losing 25 critical marks that decide the final merit ranking!

## 2. RBI Assistant & NABARD Computer Proficiency Test (CPT)

For posts in the **Reserve Bank of India (RBI Assistant)** and **NABARD Development Assistant**, shortlisted candidates must clear a qualifying Computer Proficiency Test:

### 🔹 Module 1: Word Processing (Typing & Formatting)
* **Task**: Type an unformatted legal or administrative text of 200 to 300 words within 10 to 15 minutes.
* **Formatting Requirements**:
  - Apply paragraph indentation and justify alignment.
  - Apply Bold, Italics, and Underlines to specified headings.
  - Insert bullets, numbering, or a simple 3x3 data table.
  - Set margins (1 inch all sides) and line spacing (1.5 lines).

### 🔹 Module 2: Spreadsheets (MS Excel)
* Construct a table with given financial or statistical figures.
* Calculate totals, averages, and percentages using basic Excel formulas (`SUM`, `AVERAGE`, `COUNT`).

## 3. Recommended Typing Strategy for Banking Aspirants

Even if your target exam does not feature a dedicated typing cutoff, mastering touch typing delivers massive advantages:

1. **Target 35 WPM for Descriptive Papers**: Training up to 35 WPM allows you to finish typing your SBI/IBPS descriptive essay and letter in just 15 minutes, leaving a full 15 minutes to review and polish your vocabulary.
2. **Practice on Standard QWERTY Desktop Keyboards**: Bank exam centers use standard USB membrane keyboards (Dell KB216, HP, Lenovo).
