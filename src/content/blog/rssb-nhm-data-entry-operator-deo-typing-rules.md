The **Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSSB / RSMSSB)** conducts recruitment for **Data Entry Operators (DEO)** under the **National Health Mission (NHM - Rajasthan)**.

Recruited personnel manage patient records, health analytics, and MIS portals across community health centres (CHCs) and district hospitals. Below are the official typing test rules and speed guidelines.

## 1. Bilingual Typing Format

Unlike exams where candidates choose only one medium, RSSB NHM DEO tests proficiency in **both Hindi and English**:
* **Session 1 (Hindi)**: 15 minutes typing on DevLys 010 / Mangal Remington Gail.
* **Session 2 (English)**: 15 minutes typing on standard English QWERTY keyboard.
* Candidates must pass both sections individually to be eligible for appointment.
