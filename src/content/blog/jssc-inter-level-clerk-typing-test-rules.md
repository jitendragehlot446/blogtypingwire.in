The **Jharkhand Staff Selection Commission (JSSC)** conducts the **Jharkhand Combined Inter Level (Clerk / Typist) Competitive Examination (JCKCCE)** for Lower Division Clerk, Typist, and Stenographer positions in state government departments.

The computer typing test in JSSC has one of the strictest accuracy standards in India, mandating that candidates keep mistakes strictly within **2%**. Below are the official rules and guidelines.

## 1. The Strict 2% Error Limit Explained

Under official JSSC evaluation rules:
* In a 250-word master passage, a candidate is allowed a maximum of **only 5 mistakes** (`250 × 0.02 = 5`).
* If a candidate commits **6 mistakes or more**, they are automatically declared **Failed / Disqualified**, even if their typing speed was 35+ WPM.
* Because every spelling omission, wrong character, or space counts toward the 2% cap, candidates must prioritize accuracy above raw speed.

---

## 2. Font & Key Mapping Rules (KrutiDev 010)

* **Legacy Font**: JSSC uses non-Unicode **KrutiDev 010**. 
* **Character Formations**: Candidates must be well-practiced in typing matras, half-letters (halant), conjunct letters (संयुक्त अक्षर), and punctuation marks without Unicode auto-correction.
* **Practice Strategy**: Practice on actual government notifications and office order passages formatted in KrutiDev 010.

## Worked Net Speed & Error Calculation Example

Understanding how JSSC Inter Level (JCKCCE) converts raw keystrokes into final qualifying speed prevents last-minute surprises:

* **Step 1 — Gross Words**: Divide total keystrokes (including spaces) by 5. Suppose in **10 minutes**, you type **1350 keystrokes** (270 gross words).
* **Step 2 — Mistake Penalty**: Suppose you commit **4 total mistakes** (each full mistake = 1 word; each half mistake = 0.5 word).
* **Step 3 — Net Speed**: Subtract mistakes from gross words (or apply the board's penalty rule) and divide by test duration. Your net qualifying speed comes to approximately **26.6 WPM**, keeping you safely above the **25 WPM Hindi** cutoff.

## Common Disqualification Traps & Exam Hall Checklist

1. **Primary Disqualification Risk**: Exceeding 5 total mistakes (2% of 250 words) causes instant disqualification even at 35+ WPM.
2. **Font & Layout Verification (KrutiDev 010)**: Always verify your selected keyboard layout during the 2-minute mock warm-up before the live timer starts.
3. **Backspace Discipline**: While backspace is permitted, retyping an entire sentence wastes 8–10 seconds. Fix immediate typos within 1–2 characters and keep moving forward.
4. **Line-Skip Prevention**: Keep your eyes 1–2 words ahead of your fingers on the upper passage pane so auto-scrolling never causes a skipped line.
5. **Hardware Readiness**: Test centers typically provide standard USB membrane keyboards (Dell/HP/TVS). Practice at least 50% of your drills on a standard membrane keyboard to build finger stamina.
