The **Jharkhand Staff Selection Commission (JSSC)** conducts the **Jharkhand Combined Inter Level (Clerk / Typist) Competitive Examination (JCKCCE)** for Lower Division Clerk, Typist, and Stenographer positions in state government departments.

The computer typing test in JSSC has one of the strictest accuracy standards in India, mandating that candidates keep mistakes strictly within **2%**. Below are the official rules and guidelines.

## 1. The Strict 2% Error Limit Explained

Under official JSSC evaluation rules:
* In a 250-word master passage, a candidate is allowed a maximum of **only 5 mistakes** (`250 × 0.02 = 5`).
* If a candidate commits **6 mistakes or more**, they are automatically declared **Failed / Disqualified**, even if their typing speed was 35+ WPM.
* Because every spelling omission, wrong character, or space counts toward the 2% cap, candidates must prioritize accuracy above raw speed.

---

## 2. Font & Key Mapping Rules (KrutiDev 010)

* **Legacy Font**: JSSC uses non-Unicode **KrutiDev 010**. 
* **Character Formations**: Candidates must be well-practiced in typing matras, half-letters (halant), conjunct letters (संयुक्त अक्षर), and punctuation marks without Unicode auto-correction.
* **Practice Strategy**: Practice on actual government notifications and office order passages formatted in KrutiDev 010.
