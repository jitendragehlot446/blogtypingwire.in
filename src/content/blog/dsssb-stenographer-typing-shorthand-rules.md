The **Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board (DSSSB)** recruits **Stenographers (Grade-III), Junior Stenographers, and Personal Assistants** across various departments, local bodies, and autonomous boards under the Government of NCT of Delhi.

Candidates qualifying the Tier-1 written examination must clear the comprehensive **Shorthand Skill Test and Computer Typing Test**. Below are the official rules and guidelines.

---

## ⚡ Quick Exam Snapshot (TL;DR Fact Matrix)

| Parameter | English Stenographer Standard | Hindi Stenographer Standard |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Shorthand Dictation Speed** | **80 Words Per Minute (WPM)** | **80 Words Per Minute (WPM)** |
| **Dictation Duration** | **10 Minutes (800 Words)** | **10 Minutes (800 Words)** |
| **Computer Typing Speed** | **40 Words Per Minute (WPM)** | **30 Words Per Minute (WPM)** |
| **Transcription Time on PC** | **40 Minutes** | **55 Minutes** |
| **Mandatory Font** | Standard English Font | Mangal Unicode (Inscript / Remington GAIL) |
| **Permissible Mistakes** | **5% (UR) / 7% (Reserved)** | **5% (UR) / 7% (Reserved)** |
| **Test Nature** | **Qualifying Phase** | **Qualifying Phase** |

## 1. Evaluation Algorithm for Transcription

* **Full Mistakes (1.0)**: Word omissions, substitution of wrong words, and unauthorized additions.
* **Half Mistakes (0.5)**: Spelling errors, punctuation discrepancies, and capitalization slips.
