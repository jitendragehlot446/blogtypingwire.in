The **High Court of Andhra Pradesh (Amaravati)** and the **High Court for the State of Telangana (Hyderabad)** recruit **Typists, Copyists, and Junior Assistants** under the **Judicial Ministerial Services**.

Due to the voluminous nature of case depositions, certified copy preparation, and cause-list drafting, the judicial ministerial service maintains one of the highest speed benchmarks in India: **45 Words Per Minute in English**. Below are the official rules and guidelines.

---

## ⚡ Quick Exam Snapshot (TL;DR Fact Matrix)

| Parameter | Official Judicial Ministerial Service Standard |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Recruitment Posts** | Typist, Copyist, Junior Assistant |
| **Mandatory Medium** | **English on Computer** |
| **Required Speed Benchmark** | **45 Words Per Minute (WPM)** |
| **Key Depressions Equivalent** | **2,250 Keystrokes in 10 Minutes** |
| **Test Duration** | **10 Minutes** |
| **Total Words in Passage** | **Approx. 450 Words** |
| **Permissible Error Cutoff** | **Maximum 5% Permissible Errors** |
| **Technical Certificate** | **Typewriting Higher Grade in English (45 WPM)** |
| **Test Nature** | **Qualifying Phase** |
| **Backspace Permitted** | **Yes (Full Editing Enabled)** |

## 1. High Speed Requirement (45 WPM) Strategy

* **High WPM Demand**: At 45 WPM, fingers must move smoothly with consistent touch-typing technique.
* **Practice Benchmark**: Candidates should consistently practice at **50 to 55 WPM at home** on legal passages to account for exam hall keyboard resistance.
