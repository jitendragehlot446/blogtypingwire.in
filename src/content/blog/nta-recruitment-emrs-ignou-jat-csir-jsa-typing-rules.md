The **National Testing Agency (NTA)** conducts typing examinations for **EMRS (Eklavya Model Residential Schools), IGNOU, CSIR, Visva-Bharati, and Central Universities**.

---

## 1. Exam Overview & Parameters

| Exam / University | Post | Duration | English Speed | Hindi Speed |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **EMRS Recruitment** | Junior Secretariat Assistant (JSA) | 10 Minutes | **35 WPM** | **30 WPM** |
| **IGNOU Non-Teaching** | Junior Assistant-cum-Typist (JAT) | 10 Minutes | **35 WPM** | **30 WPM** |
| **CSIR Recruitment** | Junior Secretariat Assistant (JSA) | 10 Minutes | **35 WPM** | **30 WPM** |
| **Central Universities (DU, etc.)**| Junior Assistant / LDC | 10 Minutes | **35 WPM** | **30 WPM** |
