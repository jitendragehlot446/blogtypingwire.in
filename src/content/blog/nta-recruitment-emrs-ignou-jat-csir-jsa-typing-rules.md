## 1. NTA Typing Test Software Mechanics

The NTA browser-based testing portal provides:
1. **Split-Screen Interface**: Master passage on top panel; candidate input textarea on bottom panel.
2. **Current Word Highlighting**: The active word is highlighted with background contrast, preventing line skips.
3. **Infinite Backspace**: Candidates can backspace freely within the active line and previous words.
4. **Passage Auto-Scroll**: As lines are completed, the passage scrolls smoothly to keep the next lines in view.

---

## 2. Speed Evaluation Formula & Mistake Penalty

$$\text{Gross Words} = \frac{\text{Total Key Depressions Typed}}{5}$$

$$\text{Net Speed (WPM)} = \frac{\text{Gross Words} - \text{Mistakes Penalty}}{\text{10 Minutes}}$$

* **Permissible Error Cutoff**: Candidates must maintain **93% to 95% net accuracy** to pass the qualifying threshold.
