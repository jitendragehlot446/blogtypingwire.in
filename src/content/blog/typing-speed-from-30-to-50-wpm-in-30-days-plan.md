Most candidates preparing for government typing tests (SSC CGL DEST, CHSL LDC, Railway NTPC, or Court Clerks) plateau between **28 and 35 WPM**. When exam notifications are announced with only 30 days remaining, panic sets in.

Haphazardly typing for 4 hours a day will only cause wrist strain and reinforce bad typing habits. What you need is **Deliberate Neurological Training**: a structured, 4-week progressive training protocol designed to take your fingers from **30 WPM to 50+ WPM** with 99% accuracy.

Here is your exact **Day 1 to Day 30 study blueprint**.

## 📅 Week 1 (Days 1 to 7): Eradicate the Hunt-and-Peck Habit

The goal of Week 1 is strictly **neurological anchoring**. You must train your brain to trust your fingers without looking down at the keyboard.

* **Daily Routine (30 Minutes)**:
  - **Minutes 0 - 10**: Home row drills (`A S D F - J K L ;`). Keep your index fingers anchored on the physical bumps of `F` and `J`.
  - **Minutes 10 - 20**: Top row (`Q W E R T - Y U I O P`) and bottom row (`Z X C V B - N M , . /`) reach drills. Reach for distant keys by extending individual fingers while keeping wrists stationary.
  - **Minutes 20 - 30**: Practice the [Top 200 Most Repeated Words in Typing Tests](/blog/most-repeated-words-in-ssc-high-court-typing-test/) at a slow, comfortable tempo of 25 to 30 WPM.
* **Golden Rule of Week 1**: Zero Backspace tolerance. If you hit a wrong key, do not backspace; keep moving forward to preserve your mental rhythm.

---

## 📅 Week 2 (Days 8 to 14): Conquering Capitalization & Punctuation

Most typists lose 10 to 15 WPM the moment a sentence contains commas, periods, quotation marks, or capital letters.

* **Daily Routine (35 Minutes)**:
  - **Minutes 0 - 15**: Dual Shift Key drills. Never use the left shift key for letters on the left side of the keyboard! Train your right pinky to press Right Shift when capitalizing `Q, W, E, R, T, A, S, D, F, Z, X, C`.
  - **Minutes 15 - 25**: Numeric & punctuation drills (`1-9`, commas, semicolons, hyphens).
  - **Minutes 25 - 35**: Type short 3-minute paragraphs from editorial newspapers (The Hindu, Indian Express) containing heavy numbers and proper nouns.
* **Milestone Benchmark**: By Day 14, your typing speed on complex texts must consistently exceed **38 to 42 WPM** with 98% accuracy.

---

## 📅 Week 3 (Days 15 to 21): Speed Bursting & Bigram Reflexes

In Week 3, you teach your motor cortex to type in **word chunks** rather than letter-by-letter:

* **Daily Routine (40 Minutes)**:
  - **Minutes 0 - 10**: Speed Bursting. Choose 5 short common words (e.g., `there, which, about, could, would`). Type each word as fast as humanly possible for 15 seconds, followed by 5 seconds of rest.
  - **Minutes 10 - 25**: Two timed 5-minute practice sessions on [English Typing Practice Passages (300 to 500 Words)](/blog/english-typing-test-passages-practice-300-500-words/).
  - **Minutes 25 - 40**: Error analysis. Identify which specific keys you missed and practice 50 words featuring those bottleneck letters.
* **Milestone Benchmark**: Your speed should touch **45 to 48 WPM** on standard passages.

---

## 📅 Week 4 (Days 22 to 30): Full TCS iON Exam Hall Simulation

During the final 8 days, replicate exact government test center parameters to eliminate exam-day shock:

* **Daily Routine (45 Minutes)**:
  - **Use an External USB Membrane Keyboard**: Put away your laptop or fancy mechanical keyboard. Connect a budget membrane keyboard (Dell KB216 or Logitech K120) with standard 4mm key travel.
  - **Disable Highlighting & Auto-Scroll**: Practice on **[TypingWire.in](https://typingwire.in)** in full TCS iON exam simulation mode.
  - **Simulate Timed Exam Sessions**:
    - **Session 1**: One 10-minute exam mock (SSC CHSL standard - 1750 key depressions).
    - **Session 2**: One 15-minute exam mock (SSC CGL DEST standard - 2000 key depressions).
  - **Cold Finger & Shivering Routine**: Do 2 minutes of wrist stretches before typing to prevent nerve tremors. Read our guide on [How to Stop Hand Shivering During Typing Tests](/blog/how-to-stop-hand-shivering-during-typing-test/).
