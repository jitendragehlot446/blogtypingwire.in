The **Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission (UPPSC)** conducts the coveted **Review Officer (RO) / Samiksha Adhikari** and **Assistant Review Officer (ARO) / Sahayak Samiksha Adhikari** examination for premier administrative postings in the **UP Secretariat (Sachivalaya), UP Revenue Board, and UPPSC Office**.

For the post of **ARO**, qualifying the computer typing test in Hindi is a strict eligibility mandate. Here is the complete breakdown of the official UPPSC typing notification rules.

## 1. Official Font & Keyboard Layout Rules

* **KrutiDev 010 Standard**: Unlike central exams (SSC) which use Mangal Unicode, UPPSC conducts the ARO typing examination on the **KrutiDev 010** font.
* **Remington Layout**: The key mapping replicates traditional mechanical Hindi typewriters. Characters like halants, matras, and conjuncts must be typed using standard KrutiDev key combinations and Alt-codes.
* **Alt-Codes Usage**: Official UPPSC passages are carefully vetted to minimize complex Alt-codes, but candidates must be familiar with common codes such as `Alt + 0216` (क्र) or `Alt + 0170` (ट्र).

---

## 2. Mistake Deduction & Qualifying Criteria

1. **Total Stroke / Word Evaluation**:
   $$\text{Gross Speed (WPM)} = \frac{\text{Total Characters Typed} / 5}{5 \text{ Minutes}}$$
2. **Permissible Error Percentage**: Candidates must maintain a high level of accuracy. Typically, errors exceeding 5% lead to disqualification.
3. **Short 5-Minute Window**: Because the test is only 5 minutes long, even a single 15-second pause or finger slip can lower your net speed below 25 WPM. Candidates must begin typing smoothly from the very first second.

---

## 3. UPPSC APS (Additional Private Secretary) Steno-Typing Rules

For the **UPPSC APS (Apar Niji Sachiv)** post:
* **Hindi Shorthand (Stenography)**: 80 WPM dictation for 5 minutes (400 words).
* **Hindi Computer Typing**: 25 WPM speed test in KrutiDev 010 for 5 minutes.
* **Computer Practical Test**: 50-mark practical test covering MS Word, MS Excel, PowerPoint, email, and web browsing.
