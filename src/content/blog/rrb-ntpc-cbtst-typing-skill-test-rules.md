The **Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) NTPC Computer Based Typing Skill Test (CBTST)** is one of the most critical qualifying stages in the Indian Railways recruitment process. Conducted on the **TCS iON digital examination platform**, the CBTST evaluates candidates on both speed and strict typographical accuracy under standardized examination parameters.

This comprehensive guide details everything you need to know about the **RRB NTPC CBTST 2026** examination—including posts requiring the test, English vs Hindi guidelines, font selections, backspace mechanics, mistake calculation formulas, and scoring case studies.

---

## 1. RRB NTPC Posts Requiring CBTST

Typing skill tests are mandatory **only for specific clerical and secretarial posts** across Graduate (Pay Level 5) and Under-Graduate (Pay Level 2) categories:

| Pay Level | Post Name | Qualifying Nature |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Level 5 (Graduate)** | **Senior Clerk cum Typist** | Qualifying (Marks not added to merit) |
| **Level 5 (Graduate)** | **Junior Accounts Assistant (JAA) cum Typist** | Qualifying (Marks not added to merit) |
| **Level 5 (Graduate)** | **Senior Time Keeper** | Qualifying (Marks not added to merit) |
| **Level 2 (Under-Graduate)** | **Junior Clerk cum Typist** | Qualifying (Marks not added to merit) |
| **Level 2 (Under-Graduate)** | **Accounts Clerk cum Typist** | Qualifying (Marks not added to merit) |
| **Level 2 (Under-Graduate)** | **Junior Time Keeper** | Qualifying (Marks not added to merit) |

> [!NOTE]
> Posts such as **Commercial Apprentice, Station Master, Goods Train Manager (Goods Guard), and Trains Clerk** do **NOT** require a typing skill test.

## 3. Hindi Typing Font & Keyboard Layout Guidelines

Hindi typing candidates often have intense confusion regarding fonts and keyboard layouts. Indian Railways officially supports the following environments on the TCS iON test engine:

### A. Supported Hindi Fonts
1. **Kruti Dev 010**: Traditional non-Unicode Hindi font based on typewriter Remington keys.
2. **DevLys 010**: Remington typewriter layout font, widely used in state typing institutions.
3. **Mangal Font**: Official Unicode font compliant with government standards.

### B. Supported Hindi Keyboard Layouts
* **Remington (GAIL) Layout**: Emulates the mechanical Hindi typewriter layout. Available for both Kruti Dev/DevLys and Mangal Unicode font.
* **Inscript Layout**: The standardized Indian script keyboard layout designed by C-DAC and approved by the Government of India. Available under Mangal font.

### C. What About Alt Codes in Hindi?
* In RRB NTPC passages, Railway instructions specifically curate passages **without complex ASCII Alt-code combinations** (e.g., `Alt+0216` for `क्र` or `Alt+0170`).
* All conjuncts and half-characters can be formed using standard key sequences using the **Halant (`्`) key** (e.g., `क + ् + र = क्र`).

## 4. Backspace & Editing Rules (TCS iON Word-Lock System)

The RRB CBTST does **not** allow free cursor movement. The interface enforces a strict **word-locking mechanism**:

1. **Before Pressing Spacebar**: You can use Backspace to correct typing errors in the word you are currently typing.
2. **After Pressing Spacebar**: As soon as you hit the Spacebar, the software commits that word and permanently locks it. **Backspace, Delete, and Arrow navigation keys are immediately disabled** for that word.
3. **No Mouse Repositioning**: Candidates cannot click with the mouse into previously typed sentences to modify spelling errors.
4. **Skipped Word Caution**: If you accidentally skip a word and press space, do not panic and do not try to backspace. Continue typing the subsequent text accurately to avoid consecutive word-alignment penalties.

## 5. The Master Passage & Retyping Rule

The master passage provided on the screen contains a minimum of **300 words in English** or **250 words in Hindi**:

* If you type at 40 WPM, you will finish the 300-word English passage in 7.5 minutes.
* **What should you do when you reach the end?**
  * You **MUST continue typing**. Press the Spacebar or Enter key once and re-type the passage from the very first word.
  * **Why retyping is essential**: If you stop typing after 300 words, your error exemption is limited to only 15 mistakes (5% of 300). If you type 450 words, your error exemption rises to **22.5 mistakes**, giving you a significantly higher safety buffer against disqualification.

## 6. Official RRB 5% Mistake Calculation Formula

Clause 13.5 of the official CEN notification outlines the exact mathematical deduction formula applied during evaluation:

### The Calculation Steps:

> **Step 1: Mistakes Allowed (5% Quota)**  
> `Mistakes Allowed = Total Words Typed × 0.05`

> **Step 2: Excess Mistakes**  
> `Excess Mistakes = Total Mistakes Committed − Mistakes Allowed`  
> *(If Total Mistakes ≤ Mistakes Allowed, Excess Mistakes = 0)*

> **Step 3: Penalty Deductions**  
> `Penalty Words = Excess Mistakes × 10`

> **Step 4: Net Speed Calculation**  
> `Net Speed (WPM) = (Total Words Typed − Penalty Words) ÷ 10 Minutes`

## 7. Comparative Case Studies: Passed vs Disqualified

To understand how heavily the **10x deduction penalty** impacts your result, study these practical scenarios:

### Scenario A: English Test (Typing 350 Words with 15 Mistakes)
* **Total Words Typed**: 350
* **5% Error Quota**: 350 × 0.05 = **17.5 mistakes allowed**
* **Mistakes Committed**: 15
* **Excess Mistakes**: 0 (15 is less than 17.5)
* **Penalty Deductions**: 0 words
* **Net Words**: 350 − 0 = 350 words
* **Net Speed**: 350 ÷ 10 = **35.0 WPM**  
* **Result**: **QUALIFIED (Cutoff: 30 WPM)**

### Scenario B: English Test (Typing 350 Words with 22 Mistakes)
* **Total Words Typed**: 350
* **5% Error Quota**: 350 × 0.05 = **17.5 mistakes allowed**
* **Mistakes Committed**: 22
* **Excess Mistakes**: 22 − 17.5 = **4.5 mistakes**
* **Penalty Deductions**: 4.5 × 10 = **45 words deducted**
* **Net Words**: 350 − 45 = 305 words
* **Net Speed**: 305 ÷ 10 = **30.5 WPM**  
* **Result**: **QUALIFIED (Borderline Pass above 30 WPM)**

### Scenario C: English Test (Typing 350 Words with 26 Mistakes)
* **Total Words Typed**: 350
* **5% Error Quota**: 350 × 0.05 = **17.5 mistakes allowed**
* **Mistakes Committed**: 26
* **Excess Mistakes**: 26 − 17.5 = **8.5 mistakes**
* **Penalty Deductions**: 8.5 × 10 = **85 words deducted**
* **Net Words**: 350 − 85 = 265 words
* **Net Speed**: 265 ÷ 10 = **26.5 WPM**  
* **Result**: **DISQUALIFIED (Below 30 WPM Cutoff)**

### Scenario D: Hindi Test (Typing 280 Words with 18 Mistakes)
* **Total Words Typed**: 280
* **5% Error Quota**: 280 × 0.05 = **14 mistakes allowed**
* **Mistakes Committed**: 18
* **Excess Mistakes**: 18 − 14 = **4 mistakes**
* **Penalty Deductions**: 4 × 10 = **40 words deducted**
* **Net Words**: 280 − 40 = 240 words
* **Net Speed**: 240 ÷ 10 = **24.0 WPM**  
* **Result**: **DISQUALIFIED (Cutoff: 25 WPM in Hindi)**

## 8. PwBD Typing Exemption Rules

Candidates with permanent physical disability can claim permanent exemption from the CBTST subject to official medical verification:

1. **Eligible Disabilities**: Blindness/Low Vision, Cerebral Palsy, and Locomotor Disability with minimum 40% permanent impairment affecting typing capability.
2. **Medical Certificate Requirement**: Candidates must upload and submit a valid Medical Certificate in the prescribed format (**Annexure VIII** of CEN Notification) issued by a recognized Medical Board comprising an Orthopedic / Eye specialist.
3. **Merit Determination**: For exempted PwBD candidates, their final merit is based entirely on their CBT-2 scores.

## 9. Top 5 Exam-Hall Tips for RRB NTPC CBTST

1. **Use the 1-Minute Mock Wisely**: Test all keyboard keys—especially the Spacebar, Enter key, and Backspace. If any key feels sticky or stiff, request an immediate keyboard replacement before the official 10-minute countdown starts.
2. **Focus on Rhythm Over Sprinting**: Because each excess mistake costs 10 words, typing at 35 WPM with 96% accuracy is vastly safer than typing at 45 WPM with 88% accuracy.
3. **Practice with Locked Backspace**: Do not practice on ordinary word processors where backspace is fully open. Use the dedicated RRB exam mode on [TypingWire.in](https://typingwire.in) where the word-lock algorithm is enabled.
4. **Never Leave Time on the Clock**: If you complete the master text in 8 minutes, immediately cycle and retype from the beginning.
5. **Keep Wrists Floating**: Rest your forearms slightly above the table surface to prevent wrist fatigue during continuous 10-minute sessions.
