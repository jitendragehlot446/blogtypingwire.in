The **Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) NTPC Computer Based Typing Skill Test (CBTST)** is mandatory for posts like **Senior Clerk cum Typist, Junior Accounts Assistant cum Typist, Senior Time Keeper, and Junior Clerk cum Typist**.

---

## 1. RRB NTPC CBTST Speed & Medium Specifications

| Parameter | English CBTST | Hindi CBTST |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Duration** | 10 Minutes | 10 Minutes |
| **Passage Length** | Minimum 300 Words | Minimum 250 Words |
| **Net Speed Required** | **30 WPM** | **25 WPM** |
| **Error Tolerance** | 5% of Total Words Typed | 5% of Total Words Typed |
| **Keyboard / Font** | Standard QWERTY | KrutiDev 010 / DevLys / Mangal |
| **Editing / Backspace** | **Disabled (Locked)** | **Disabled (Locked)** |
| **Retyping Feature** | Permitted after reaching end | Permitted after reaching end |

---

## 2. The Official RRB 5% Error Penalty Formula

$$\text{Mistakes Allowed (5\% Limit)} = \text{Total Words Typed} \times 0.05$$

$$\text{Excess Mistakes} = \text{Total Mistakes} - \text{Mistakes Allowed}$$

$$\text{Penalty Words} = \text{Excess Mistakes} \times 10$$

$$\text{Net Speed (WPM)} = \frac{\text{Total Words Typed} - \text{Penalty Words}}{10 \text{ Minutes}}$$

### 📊 Calculation Example:
If a candidate types **400 words** in 10 minutes and makes **24 mistakes**:
1. 5% of 400 = **20 mistakes are completely exempt/ignored**.
2. Excess Mistakes = $24 - 20 = \mathbf{4 \text{ mistakes}}$.
3. Penalty Words = $4 \times 10 = \mathbf{40 \text{ words deducted}}$.
4. Net Words = $400 - 40 = \mathbf{360 \text{ words}}$.
5. Net Speed = $360 / 10 = \mathbf{36 \text{ WPM}}$ $\rightarrow$ **QUALIFIED (Above 30 WPM)!**
