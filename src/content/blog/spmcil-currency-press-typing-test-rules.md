The **Security Printing and Minting Corporation of India Limited (SPMCIL)** is a Schedule 'A' Miniratna Category-I Central Public Sector Enterprise (CPSE) operating under the Ministry of Finance. It manages vital security institutions including the **Currency Note Press (CNP Nashik)**, **Bank Note Press (BNP Dewas)**, **Security Printing Press (SPP Hyderabad)**, and **India Government Mints (Mumbai, Kolkata, Hyderabad, Noida)**.

For recruitment to the post of **Junior Office Assistant (JOA) at W-3 Level**, clearing the computer typing test is mandatory. Below are the official rules and guidelines.

---

## ⚡ Quick Exam Snapshot (TL;DR Fact Matrix)

| Parameter | English Typing Standard | Hindi Typing Standard |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Minimum Required Speed** | **35 Words Per Minute (WPM)** | **30 Words Per Minute (WPM)** |

| **Test Duration** | **10 Minutes** | **10 Minutes** |
| **Passage Length** | **Approx. 350 Words (1,750 Keystrokes)** | **Approx. 300 Words (1,500 Keystrokes)** |
| **Font Standard** | Standard English Font | Mangal Unicode (Inscript / Remington GAIL) |
| **Permissible Error Limit** | **Maximum 5% Errors Allowed** | **Maximum 5% Errors Allowed** |
| **Test Nature** | **Qualifying Only** | **Qualifying Only** |
| **Backspace Permitted** | **Yes (Full Editing Enabled)** | **Yes (Full Editing Enabled)** |

## 1. Official Speed Evaluation Formula

* **Standard Word**: Calculated on the basis of 5 key depressions per word.
* **Speed Formula**:
  $$\text{Net Speed (WPM)} = \frac{\text{Total Keystrokes Typed} - (\text{Mistake Penalty} \times 5)}{5 \times 10 \text{ Minutes}}$$
* Candidates must achieve the requisite 35 WPM (English) or 30 WPM (Hindi) net speed to clear the phase.

## 2. Examination Hall Preparation Tips

* **Official Banking & Finance Passages**: SPMCIL passages frequently feature economic, administrative, and currency terminology containing numbers and capital letters.
* **Consistent Pace**: Ensure steady pacing to avoid running out of time on the final paragraph.
