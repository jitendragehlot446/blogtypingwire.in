The **Indian Army** has modernized its recruitment pipeline for **Agniveer Clerk and Store Keeper Technical (SKT)** by introducing a mandatory **Computer-Based Typing Test** directly during the initial testing phase.

Candidates must clear this online skill test to qualify for the physical fitness test (PFT) and final merit list. Below are the official guidelines, speed benchmarks, and evaluation criteria based on the latest Indian Army notification.

## 1. Official Speed & Accuracy Requirements

Under the updated Agniveer recruitment scheme:
* **Minimum Speed**: You must type at a speed of at least **30 WPM**.
* **Keystrokes Calculation**: A standard word is evaluated at **5 key depressions**. Therefore, typing 30 WPM requires generating at least **1,500 keystrokes in 10 minutes**.
* **Accuracy Threshold**: Candidates must achieve **at least 96% accuracy**. If your speed is 35 WPM but your accuracy falls below 96%, you will be disqualified.

---

## 2. Test Software Interface & Evaluation Rules

The Army CEE typing software follows standardized testing rules:
1. **On-Screen Passage**: The master paragraph is displayed on the computer screen. Candidates read from the upper text box and type into the lower text editor.
2. **Live Countdown Timer**: A 10:00-minute timer runs at the top-right corner and auto-submits when time expires.
3. **Punctuation & Capitalization**: Punctuation marks (commas, full stops, apostrophes) and capital letters are strictly checked against the master text.
4. **Omission / Addition Penalty**: Skipping entire words or inserting additional random words directly penalizes your net accuracy score.

---

## 3. Preparation Strategy for Agniveer Clerk Aspirants

* **Practice 300-Word English Passages**: Focus on 10-minute continuous typing sessions to develop muscle memory.
* **Maintain 98% Accuracy in Practice**: Do not chase 45 WPM with poor accuracy; consistent 35 WPM with zero errors ensures a 100% pass rate.
* **Get Used to Desktop Keyboards**: Most Army testing labs use membrane keyboards with standard key travel. Avoid practicing solely on slim laptop keyboards.
