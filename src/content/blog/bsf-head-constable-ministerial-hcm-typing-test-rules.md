The **Border Security Force (BSF)** recruits **Head Constable (Ministerial) / HCM** and **ASI (Stenographer)** across frontier headquarters, sector headquarters, and battalion units.

The Phase-II skill test is a rigorous qualifying milestone. Below are the official rules, speed criteria, and evaluation formulas.

---

## ⚡ Quick Exam Snapshot (TL;DR Fact Matrix)

| Parameter | English Typing Standard | Hindi Typing Standard |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Minimum Required Speed** | **35 Words Per Minute (WPM)** | **30 Words Per Minute (WPM)** |
| **Key Depressions (KDPH)** | **10,500 KDPH on Computer** | **9,000 KDPH on Computer** |
| **Test Duration** | **10 Minutes** | **10 Minutes** |
| **Required Keystrokes** | **Approx. 1,750 Keystrokes in 10 Min** | **Approx. 1,500 Keystrokes in 10 Min** |
| **Font Standard** | Standard English Font | Mangal Unicode (Inscript / Remington GAIL) |
| **Passage Format** | **Printed Hard Copy Document** | **Printed Hard Copy Document** |
| **Test Nature** | **Qualifying Only** | **Qualifying Only** |
| **Backspace Permitted** | **Yes (Standard Editing Allowed)** | **Yes (Standard Editing Allowed)** |

---

## 1. Official Speed Calculation & Keystroke Conversion

* **Standard Conversion**: 1 standard word = 5 characters / key depressions.
* **Calculation Formula**:
  `Net Speed (WPM) = (Correct Keystrokes / 5 / 10 Minutes)`
* Candidates who type the complete 350-word master passage with high accuracy secure qualifying status comfortably.
