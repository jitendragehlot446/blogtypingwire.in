## 1. The Core Fundamentals: Touch Typing Without Looking

Typing with two index fingers while looking down at the keyboard caps your speed at 20 WPM permanently. To achieve 40+ WPM, you must train your 10 fingers using **muscle memory**:

1. **The Anchor Keys**:
   * Left Hand: Rest fingers on `A - S - D - F` (Remington: *ं, े, ्, ि* \| Inscript: *ो, े, ्, ि*).
   * Right Hand: Rest fingers on `J - K - L - ;` (Remington: *र, क, त, च* \| Inscript: *र, क, त, च*).
   * Thumbs: Rest lightly on the Spacebar.
2. **The "Eyes on Screen" Rule**: Tape a paper over your hands or cover your keyboard for 15 minutes daily. Never look down. If you make a mistake, let your fingers locate the key by feel.

## 2. Conquering the 3 Hardest Hindi Typing Challenges

### 🅰️ Challenge 1: The 'Chhoti I' Matra (ि) Rule
On modern Unicode Remington Gail, students often press the matra key before the letter (as done on manual typewriters), causing input errors.
* **The Unicode Rule**: Always type the **Consonant first**, followed by the **Matra key**.
* Example: To type **'कि'**, press `K` (क) then `F` (ि). The computer automatically reorders the visual glyph!

### 🅱️ Challenge 2: Half-Letters & Conjuncts (*संयुक्त अक्षर*)
In Hindi exams, passages are packed with words like *न्यायालय, प्रशासन, दृष्टिकोण, उत्तरदायित्व, संविधान*.
* On Remington Gail: Half letter is formed by pressing the letter key followed by `D` (Halant).
* Example for **'क्य'**: Press `K` (क) + `D` (्) + `;` (य).

### 🅲 Challenge 3: Punctuation & Special Symbols
* **Poorna Viram ( | )**: In Remington Gail, press `Shift + A`.
* **Inverted Commas (" ")**: Memorize the shift combinations to avoid pausing during speech quotations.

## 3. The "No-Backspace" Drill: The Secret to Explosive Speed

Why do candidates get stuck at 22 WPM?  
Because every time they make a typo, they hit **Backspace 3 to 5 times**, recalculate their position, and retype. This costs **3.5 seconds per error**. In a 10-minute test, 20 backspaces waste **70 seconds (over 1 minute of total test time!)**.

### The Drill:
1. Spend 15 minutes daily typing on [TypingWire.in](https://typingwire.in) with **Backspace strictly disabled**.
2. Force your fingers to type at a controlled, rhythmic pace where errors are nearly impossible.
3. Once your raw accuracy crosses 97%, your net speed will jump by 8 to 10 WPM automatically.
