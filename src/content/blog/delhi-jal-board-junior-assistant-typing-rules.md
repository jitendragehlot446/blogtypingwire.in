The **Delhi Jal Board (DJB)**, operating under the Government of NCT of Delhi, recruits **Junior Assistants and Lower Division Clerks** through the **Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board (DSSSB)**.

Candidates shortlisted from the Tier-1 written examination must qualify the mandatory skill test. Below are the official rules and guidelines.

---

## ⚡ Quick Exam Snapshot (TL;DR Fact Matrix)

| Parameter | English Typing Standard | Hindi Typing Standard |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Minimum Required Speed** | **35 Words Per Minute (WPM)** | **30 Words Per Minute (WPM)** |
| **Key Depressions (KDPH)** | **10,500 KDPH on Computer** | **9,000 KDPH on Computer** |
| **Duration of Test** | **10 Minutes** | **10 Minutes** |
| **Required Keystrokes** | **1,750 Keystrokes ($\pm 5\%$)** | **1,500 Keystrokes ($\pm 5\%$)** |
| **Recruitment Body** | **DSSSB (Govt. of NCT of Delhi)** | **DSSSB (Govt. of NCT of Delhi)** |
| **Font Standard** | Standard English Font | Mangal Unicode (Inscript / Remington GAIL) |
| **Test Nature** | **Qualifying Only** | **Qualifying Only** |
| **Backspace Permitted** | **Yes (Full Editing Enabled)** | **Yes (Full Editing Enabled)** |

---

## 1. Speed Evaluation & Retyping Rules

* In accordance with DSSSB exam regulations, if a candidate finishes typing the entire passage before the 10-minute timer expires, they should **retype the passage from the beginning** to maximize keystroke volume.
* Net speed is evaluated on correct character count divided by 5, divided by 10 minutes.
