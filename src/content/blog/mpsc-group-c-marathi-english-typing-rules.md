The **Maharashtra Public Service Commission (MPSC)** conducts computer typing skill tests for **Group 'C' Clerk-Typist (Marathi & English) and Tax Assistant**.

---

## 1. Exam Specifications & Speed Criteria

| Medium | Duration | Words in Passage | Target Speed | Permissible Error Limit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Marathi Typing** | 10 Minutes | 300 Words | **30 WPM** | **5% Error Tolerance** |
| **English Typing** | 10 Minutes | 400 Words | **40 WPM** | **5% Error Tolerance** |
