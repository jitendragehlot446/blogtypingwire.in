The **Data Entry Speed Test (DEST)** is a mandatory qualifying paper for all posts in the **SSC CGL examination**. Candidates who clear the computer-based Tier-II examination must qualify the typing test to be considered for final merit allocation.

In this official guide, we break down the exact speed calculation formula, permissible mistake percentage, and keyboard settings required to clear SSC CGL DEST with 100% confidence.

---

## 1. SSC CGL DEST Speed & Duration Specifications

| Parameter | English DEST | Hindi DEST |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Duration** | 15 Minutes | 15 Minutes |
| **Target Key Depressions** | 2000 Keystrokes ($\pm 5\%$) | 2000 Keystrokes ($\pm 5\%$) |
| **Effective Word Speed** | ~27 WPM (Words Per Minute) | ~30 WPM (Words Per Minute) |
| **Word Count in Passage** | Approx 350 - 400 Words | Approx 350 - 400 Words |
| **Keyboard Layout** | Standard QWERTY | Mangal Inscript / Remington Gail |
| **Nature of Paper** | Qualifying (Mandatory) | Qualifying (Mandatory) |

## 2. Category-Wise Permissible Error Percentage

SSC evaluates DEST errors on two levels: Standard qualifying limit and Module-specific higher cutoff for specific departments (e.g. Tax Assistant in CBDT/CBIC, Executive Assistant).

| Category | Standard Qualifying Limit | Higher Cutoff Posts (Tax Assistant, etc.) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **UR / General** | 20% Max Error | **5% Max Error** |
| **OBC / EWS** | 25% Max Error | **7% Max Error** |
| **SC / ST** | 30% Max Error | **7% Max Error** |
| **PwD (OH/HH/VH)** | 30% Max Error | **10% Max Error** |

## 3. Full Mistakes vs Half Mistakes in SSC Evaluation

SSC uses a rigorous error evaluation algorithm to compute candidate mistakes:

### 🔴 Full Mistakes (100% Penalty)
1. **Omission of Word**: Skipping any word or phrase from the master passage.
2. **Substitution of Word**: Replacing a correct master word with a wrong word.
3. **Addition of Word**: Typing extra words that do not exist in the master text.
4. **Spelling Mistake**: Every wrong spelling counts as a full mistake.

### 🟡 Half Mistakes (50% Penalty)
1. **Spacing Errors**: Leaving no space between two words or typing double spaces where a single space was required.
2. **Punctuation Errors**: Omitting or misplacing commas, full stops, quotation marks, or hyphens.
3. **Capitalization Errors**: Typing small letters where capital letters are required (e.g. `india` instead of `India`).

---

## 4. SSC Error Percentage Formula

$$\text{Total Mistakes} = \text{Full Mistakes} + \left(\frac{\text{Half Mistakes}}{2}\right)$$

$$\text{Error Percentage (\%)} = \left(\frac{\text{Total Mistakes} \times 100}{\text{Total Words in Master Passage}}\right)$$

*Example*: If the passage has 400 words, and you make 10 Full Mistakes and 6 Half Mistakes:
$$\text{Total Mistakes} = 10 + \frac{6}{2} = 13$$
$$\text{Error \%} = \frac{13 \times 100}{400} = 3.25\% \quad (\text{Qualified under 5\% limit})$$
