Every candidate who types at **50, 60, or 80+ WPM** follows one fundamental principle: **Touch Typing (Blind Typing)**.

Touch typing is the method of typing without using the sense of sight to find the keys. Instead, you rely purely on **finger muscle memory** anchored to the middle **Home Row**.

If you currently type using only two or three fingers while constantly glancing down at the keyboard, this guide will rewire your technique to unlock effortless speed.

## 1. The 3 Golden Rules of Touch Typing

### 🥇 Rule 1: Always Return to the Home Row Anchor
After your finger reaches up to strike `T` or reaches down to strike `C`, it must **immediately return to its home key (`F` or `D`)**. The home row is your home base; your fingers should never float in mid-air.

### 🥈 Rule 2: Trust the Tactile Ridges on 'F' and 'J'
Close your eyes and glide your index fingers across the keyboard. Feel the small raised plastic bar or bump on the **F key** and the **J key**. 
* Whenever you lose your orientation, find these two bumps without looking down. Your other three fingers will naturally fall on `A-S-D` and `K-L-;`.

### 🥉 Rule 3: Speed is the Byproduct of Accuracy
Never force your fingers to move faster than your brain can confirm the key position. If you make 3 typos in a row, slow down your tempo by 20%. When you type cleanly at 98% accuracy, high speed occurs automatically.

## 2. Step-by-Step 14-Day Muscle Memory Drills

### Phase 1 (Days 1 to 4): The Home Row Only
* Drill: Type repeating strings without looking:  
  `asdf jkl; asdf jkl; a s d f j k l ; sad fads fall flask salad`
* Aim for 100% accuracy before touching other rows.

### Phase 2 (Days 5 to 8): Adding the Top Row (QWERTY)
* Drill: Integrate vowels `E, I, O, U` and common consonants `T, R`:  
  `the quick brown fox red kite elite letter state trial`

### Phase 3 (Days 9 to 14): Adding the Bottom Row & Numbers
* Drill: Integrate `Z, X, C, V, B, N, M` and punctuation keys:  
  `civil, court, clerk, judge, justice, 2026, Article 14, Section 302.`
