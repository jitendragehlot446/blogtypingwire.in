The **Madhya Pradesh Agency for Promotion of Information Technology (MAP_IT)** conducts the **Computer Proficiency Certification Test (CPCT)** bi-monthly across Madhya Pradesh.

A valid CPCT scorecard is a **mandatory prerequisite** for applying to clerical and technical positions across MP government departments, including **MP Patwari, Assistant Grade-3 (AG-3), Stenographer, Data Entry Operator, and High Court / District Court staff**.

| Test Section | Subject Matter | Duration | Minimum Qualifying Score |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **Section 2: English Typing**| Standard English legal/general text | 15 Minutes | **30.0 Words Per Minute (50%)** |
| **Section 3: Hindi Typing** | Unicode Mangal on Remington Gail layout | 15 Minutes | **20.0 Words Per Minute (50%)** |

| **Backspace Allowed** | **Yes (Full Editing Enabled)** | - | Active |

## 1. CPCT Typing Speed Percentile Slabs (English & Hindi)

The MP CPCT scorecard awards percentile scoring brackets based on verified Net Speed:

### 📑 English Typing Speed Slabs (15 Minutes)
* **Below 30 WPM**: Not Qualified (0%)
* **30 to 40 WPM**: Qualified (50% Grade)
* **41 to 50 WPM**: Qualified (60% Grade)
* **51 to 60 WPM**: Qualified (70% Grade)
* **Above 60 WPM**: Qualified (80%+ Top Distinction Grade)

### 📑 Hindi Typing Speed Slabs (15 Minutes)
* **Below 20 WPM**: Not Qualified (0%)
* **20 to 25 WPM**: Qualified (50% Grade)
* **26 to 30 WPM**: Qualified (60% Grade)
* **31 to 40 WPM**: Qualified (70% Grade)
* **Above 40 WPM**: Qualified (80%+ Top Distinction Grade)

---

## 2. Technical System Specifications for Hindi Typing

* **Font**: **Mangal (Unicode Standard)**.
* **Layout**: **Remington (Gail)**. Candidates must type character-first followed by matra (e.g., `क` then `ि` for `कि`).
* Passages contain official government orders, education policy circulars, and administrative drafts.

## 3. High-Scoring Routine for MP CPCT Aspirants

1. **Practice Both Sections Daily**: Ensure you clear both the 38-mark MCQ cutoff and the typing speed cutoffs on the same exam day.
