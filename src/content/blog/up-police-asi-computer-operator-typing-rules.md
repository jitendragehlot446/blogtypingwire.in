The **Uttar Pradesh Police Recruitment and Promotion Board (UPPRPB)** conducts recruitment for **Computer Operator Grade-A, Assistant Sub-Inspector (Clerk), and ASI (Accounts)**.

Unlike exams that allow traditional Remington typewriter layouts, the UP Police skill test is famous for mandating **Unicode Mangal on Inscript keyboard layout**.

## 1. Why UP Police Mangal Inscript is Crucial

Most Hindi typists in North India learn on KrutiDev or Remington Gail. However, UPPRPB notifications specifically mandate **Inscript keyboard layout**:
* Inscript is standardized by the Government of India.
* **Layout Logic**: All vowels are grouped on the left-hand keys (*Shift + E, R, T, etc.*) and consonants on the right-hand keys (*K, L, P, etc.*).
* **Halant Rule**: Every conjunct (*क्, त्, प्*) is created by pressing the consonant followed by `D` (the Halant key).

> [!CAUTION]
> If you show up at the UP Police exam center expecting Remington Gail or KrutiDev, you will not be able to type on the Inscript layout! Start practicing Inscript at least 60 days before the exam.

## 2. Speed & Accuracy Formula (UPPRPB Algorithm)

`Gross Speed (WPM) = (Total Characters Typed / 5 / Time (15 Minutes))`

`Accuracy Percentage = ( (Correct Characters Typed / Total Characters Typed) ) × 100`

* If your Gross Speed is 35 WPM but your accuracy is **84.9%**, you are declared **FAILED**.
* If your Gross Speed is 26 WPM and your accuracy is **88%**, you are declared **QUALIFIED**.
