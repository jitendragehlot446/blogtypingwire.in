The **Uttar Pradesh Police Recruitment and Promotion Board (UPPRPB)** mandates a high-precision computer typing test for **Assistant Sub-Inspector (ASI Confidential/Clerk/Accounts)** and **Computer Operator Grade-A**.

---

## 1. Speed & Accuracy Specifications

| Post | Hindi Typing Requirement | English Typing Requirement | Duration | Nature of Test |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **ASI (Clerk)** | **25 WPM** (85% Accuracy) | **30 WPM** (85% Accuracy) | 15 Mins Each | Qualifying |
| **ASI (Accounts)** | **15 WPM** (85% Accuracy) | Not Mandatory | 15 Minutes | Qualifying |
| **Computer Operator Grade-A** | **25 WPM** (85% Accuracy) | **30 WPM** (85% Accuracy) | 15 Mins Each | Qualifying |

---

## 2. Inscript vs Remington Gail in UP Police Exams
* The exam software allows candidates to choose between **Inscript** and **Remington Gail (Mangal font)**.
* **Backspace Key**: In UP Police examinations, the Backspace key is functional, but excessive corrections eat into the 15-minute time limit.
