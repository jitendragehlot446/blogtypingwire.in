The **Official Recruitment Notification for Assistant (Group-B) in the High Court of Judicature at Patna** outlines a multi-stage selection process comprising Preliminary Test, Written Test, **Computer Proficiency Test (CPT) & Typing Test**, and Interview.

---

## ⚡ Official Notification Specifications (TL;DR Fact Matrix)

| Evaluation Component | Minimum Benchmark | Time Allotted | Qualifying Requirement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **English Typing Test** | **40 Words Per Minute** | 10 Minutes | **90% Minimum Accuracy** |
| **Hindi Typing Test** | **30 Words Per Minute** | 10 Minutes | **85% Minimum Accuracy** |
| **MS Word Practical Test** | Page setup, indents, tables, styles | 15 Minutes | Minimum 40% Marks |
| **MS Excel Practical Test** | Formulas, cell formatting, charts | 15 Minutes | Minimum 40% Marks |
| **Internet / Email Test** | Web research, email drafting | 5–10 Minutes | Minimum 40% Marks |
| **Overall CPT Nature** | Practical Computer Skills | Total ~40 Mins | **Mandatory Qualifying (40% Cutoff)** |

## 1. MS Word Practical Tasks & Marking Breakdown

Candidates are given a raw text document and an instructions sheet specifying exact formatting parameters:
1. **Page Layout Setup**: Setting page size to A4, margins to 1-inch (or Narrow 0.5-inch), orientation to Portrait.
2. **Typography & Paragraph Formatting**: Setting heading to 16pt Bold Center, body text to 12pt Times New Roman, line spacing to 1.15, text alignment to Justified (`Ctrl + J`).
3. **Table Formatting**: Inserting a 4-column by 5-row table, applying borders, shading the header row with 15% gray fill, aligning numeric data to the right and text data to the left.
4. **Header, Footer & Page Numbers**: Adding the document title in the header (Right aligned) and page number in the footer (Center aligned).

---

## 2. MS Excel Practical Tasks & Evaluation Scheme

Candidates receive raw tabular data and must execute specific formula calculations:
1. **Mathematical Operations**: Applying `=SUM(B2:B10)` for column totals and `=AVERAGE(C2:C10)` for averages.
2. **Conditional Formulas**: Utilizing `=IF(D2>=40, "Pass", "Fail")` to evaluate condition-based outcomes.
3. **Number Formatting**: Formatting currency columns as Indian Rupee (₹) or standard numeric format with two decimal places.
4. **Cell Styling**: Applying text wrapping, merging header cells, and adding all-around grid borders.
