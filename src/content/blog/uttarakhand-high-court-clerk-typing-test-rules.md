The **High Court of Uttarakhand (Nainital)** recruits **Clerks, Junior Assistants, Personal Assistants, and Stenographers** for the High Court registry and subordinate district courts across Uttarakhand.

The computer typing test and basic computer knowledge test are crucial qualifying criteria for final selection. Below are the official rules and guidelines.

---

| Parameter | Hindi Typing Standard | English Typing Standard |

## 1. Official Evaluation Method & Mistake Scoring

* **Net Word Calculation**: 
  $$\text{Net Speed (WPM)} = \frac{\text{Total Words Typed} - \text{Mistakes Penalty}}{10 \text{ Minutes}}$$
* **Passage Subject Matter**: Passages are derived from legal judgments, court notices, and government administrative correspondence.
* **Capitalization and Punctuation**: Strict adherence to commas, colons, case-sensitivity, and section references is evaluated.

---

## 2. Computer Practical Examination (CPT)

In addition to the speed test, candidates are evaluated on:
* **MS Word Formatting**: Table insertion, margins, font styling, headers, and footers.
* **MS Excel Formulas**: Basic calculation functions (`SUM`, `AVERAGE`), cell alignment, and border settings.
