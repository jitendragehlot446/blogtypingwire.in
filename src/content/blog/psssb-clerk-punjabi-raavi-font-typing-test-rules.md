The **Punjab Subordinate Services Selection Board (PSSSB, SAS Nagar Mohali)** conducts recruitment for thousands of **Clerk, Clerk IT, Clerk Accounts, and Legal Clerk** vacancies across Punjab government departments.

PSSSB is known for its rigorous **Bilingual Typing Test**, where clearing **Punjabi typing on Unicode Raavi font (Asees layout)** alongside English typing is strictly mandatory.

## 1. Punjabi Typing on Raavi Font (Asees Layout Rules)

Unlike phonetic transliteration, PSSSB mandates **Asees keyboard layout on Raavi font**:
* **Gurmukhi Glyphs**: Standard letter mapping requires memorizing key locations for Gurmukhi vowels and consonants.
* **Tippi (ੰ) & Bindi (ਂ)**: Nasal sound markers must be typed after the consonant.
* **Adhak (ੱ)**: Represents gemination/stress on consonants; must be typed before the stressed consonant in Asees layout.
* **Pairin Akhars (ਪੈਰੀਂ ਅੱਖਰ - ਰ, ਹ, ਵ)**: Subscript letters are formed using specific key combinations (`Shift` keys).

## 2. Speed & Error Deduction Formula

`Gross Speed (WPM) = (Total Words Typed / Time (10 Minutes))`

`Error Percentage (%) = ( (Total Mistakes / Total Words Typed) ) × 100`

`Net Speed (WPM) = Gross Speed - ( (Mistakes / 10 Minutes) )`

* If a candidate achieves 38 WPM gross speed but commits **8.5% mistakes**, they are declared **FAILED** due to exceeding the 8% error ceiling!
