## 1. The Infamous CRPF 10-Word Mistake Penalty Formula

The official notification lays down the mathematical evaluation formula:

`Permissible Mistakes = Total Words Typed × 0.05`

`Excess Mistakes = Total Mistakes Committed - Permissible Mistakes`

`Net Words = Total Words Typed - (Excess Mistakes × 10)`

`Net Speed (WPM) = (Net Words / 10 Minutes)`

### 🚨 Real-World Calculation Example:
* Suppose you type **400 words** in 10 minutes (Gross speed: 40 WPM).
* Permissible errors (5%) = 400 × 0.05 = 20 mistakes.
* If you committed **26 mistakes**, excess mistakes = 26 - 20 = 6 mistakes.
* Penalty deducted = 6 × 10 = 60 words.
* Net Words = 400 - 60 = 340 words.
* **Net Speed** = 340 / 10 = 34 WPM \rightarrow **DISQUALIFIED (Failed by 1 WPM)**!

Just **6 mistakes above the cutoff** wiped out 60 words and caused outright failure.

## 2. Practical Tips to Clear CRPF HCM

1. **Practice Paper-to-Screen Typing**: Place printed newspapers or book passages on the left/right side of your monitor. Do not rely solely on web software with auto-scrolling words.
2. **Never Rush the First 2 Minutes**: Hand trembling is common. Breathe deeply and start at a calm, controlled 30 WPM pace before ramping up to 40 WPM.
