The **High Court of Judicature at Allahabad** conducts one of the largest state-level judicial recruitments for **Group 'C' Clerical Cadre (Junior Assistant & Paid Apprentice)** across District Courts of Uttar Pradesh.

Unlike RO/ARO recruitments which test only English typing, the **Group C examination mandates BOTH Hindi (25 WPM) and English (30 WPM) typing**, carrying a massive **50 marks that directly decide final selection**.

## 1. Official 50-Mark Scoring & Penalty Math

In Allahabad High Court Group C, high marks in typing separate the top rankers from the rest:

`Hindi Score (Out of 25) = 25 - (Total Errors × 0.25)`

`English Score (Out of 25) = 25 - (Total Errors × 0.25)`

### Error Deductions:
* **Each Full Error (Word Omission, Word Substitution)**: Deducts **0.25 to 0.50 marks**.
* **Each Half Error (Spelling typo, punctuation mistake)**: Deducts **0.125 to 0.25 marks**.
* If your net speed falls below **25 WPM in Hindi or 30 WPM in English**, you are disqualified regardless of your written examination score!

## 2. Examination Flow on Test Day

1. **Part-A (Hindi Session)**: Candidates complete the 10-minute Hindi passage on Mangal font.
2. **Interval (5 to 10 Minutes)**: System resets, allowing candidates to adjust seating and prepare for English.
3. **Part-B (English Session)**: Candidates complete the 10-minute English 300-word passage.
