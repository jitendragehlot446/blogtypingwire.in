## 1. Step-by-Step Connection Guide

### Step 1: OTG Adapter Select Karein
* **Type-C Phones**: Aajkal ke lagbhag sabhi naye phones me Type-C charging port hota hai. Dukandar se *"Type-C to USB Female OTG"* maangein.
* **Micro-USB Phones**: Purane models ke liye *"Micro-USB OTG"* lein.

### Step 2: Keyboard Connect Karein
* OTG adapter ko phone ke charging jack me lagayein.
* Keyboard ki USB wire ko OTG ke doosre sire me lagayein.

### Step 3: Phone ki "OTG Connection" Setting ON Karein
Ye sabse critical step hai jisme 90% chhatra galti karte hain:
1. Phone ki **Settings** kholein.
2. Upar search bar me type karein: **`OTG`**.
3. **"OTG Connection"** option par click karke use **Turn ON** kar dein.
4. *Jaise hi aap switch on karenge, aapke keyboard par NumLock ya CapsLock ki green/blue light jal jayegi!*

> [!NOTE]
> Samsung aur Google Pixel phones me OTG hamesha auto-detect hota hai, unme setting on karne ki zaroorat nahi hoti. Realme, Vivo, Oppo, OnePlus aur Xiaomi phones me ise manually on karna padta hai.

## 2. Best Mobile Display Settings for Typing Exam

Apne phone par bilkul computer jaisa mahol banane ke liye ye 3 settings zaroor karein:

1. **Auto-Rotate ON Karein**:
   * Phone ko hamesha **Landscape Mode (Aada)** karke mobile stand par rakhein. Isse text chhota nahi lagega aur line poori dikhegi.
2. **Desktop Site Mode (Chrome Browser)**:
   * Chrome browser kholein aur `https://blog.typingwire.in` ya `https://typingwire.in` par jayein.
   * Upar right corner me **3 Dots (⋮)** par click karein aur **"Desktop site"** par check mark laga dein.
   * Ab aapki screen bilkul TCS iON aur NTA computer lab jaisi split-screen me badal jayegi!
3. **On-Screen Keyboard ko Chhupayein**:
   * Jab physical keyboard judta hai, to kabhi-kabhi phone ka software keyboard screen par adhi jagah gher leta hai.
   * Iske liye phone ki *Settings ➔ System ➔ Keyboard & Input ➔ Physical Keyboard ➔ "Show virtual keyboard"* ko **OFF** kar dein.
