The **Uttarakhand Public Service Commission (UKPSC)** and **UKSSSC** conduct typing and computer efficiency tests for **Review Officer (RO), Assistant Review Officer (ARO), and Additional Private Secretary (APS)**.

---

## 1. Exam Specifications & Speed Criteria

| Exam / Post | Language | Key Depressions | Duration | Font Used |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **UKPSC RO / ARO** | Hindi (Mandatory) | 4,000 KDPH (667 KD) | 10 Minutes | Mangal (Unicode) |
| **UKPSC RO / ARO** | English (Preferential) | 8,000 KDPH (1333 KD) | 10 Minutes | Standard QWERTY |
| **UKPSC APS** | Hindi / English | 2,500 / 2,750 Keys | 10 Minutes | Mangal / QWERTY |
| **Uttarakhand High Court** | Hindi / English | 250 / 300 Words | 10 Minutes | Mangal / QWERTY |
