## 1. Speed Calculation & Mathematical Formula

`Gross Words = (Total Keystrokes Typed / 5)`

`Net Speed (WPM) = (Gross Words - Mistakes Penalty / Time (5 Minutes))`

* Because the test is only **5 minutes long**, candidates must avoid hesitant starts. Pausing for even 20 seconds can cost you 4 to 6 WPM.

---

## 2. Full Mistakes vs Half Mistakes in HSSC

1. **Full Errors (1 Word Deducted)**:
   * Skipping any word from the master printed passage.
   * Substituting a printed word with an incorrect word.
   * Inserting extraneous words or figures.
2. **Half Errors (0.5 Word Deducted)**:
   * Punctuation mistakes (missing commas, periods).
   * Spacing errors (missing space or double space).
   * Capitalization inaccuracies.
