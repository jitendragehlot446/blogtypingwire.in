The **Bihar Police Subordinate Services Commission (BPSSC)** conducts recruitment for the post of **Steno Assistant Sub-Inspector (ASI) / स्टेनो सहायक अवर निरीक्षक** in the Bihar Police Department.

The selection process involves a written examination followed by a rigorous **Hindi Shorthand Dictation Test** and a **Computer Typing Test (Hindi & English)**. Below are the official rules and guidelines.

---

## ⚡ Quick Exam Snapshot (TL;DR Fact Matrix)

| Parameter | English Typing Medium | Hindi Typing Medium |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Minimum Required Speed** | **30 Words Per Minute (WPM)** | **25 Words Per Minute (WPM)** |
| **Test Duration** | **10 Minutes** | **10 Minutes** |
| **Passage Volume** | **300 Words** | **250 Words** |
| **Mandatory Font** | Standard English Font | Mangal Font (Remington GAIL / Inscript) |
| **Shorthand Benchmark** | — | **80 WPM Hindi Shorthand (5 Min Dictation)** |
| **Permissible Error Limit** | **Maximum 10% Errors Allowed** | **Maximum 10% Errors Allowed** |
| **Test Nature** | **Qualifying Phase** | **Qualifying Phase** |
| **Backspace Permitted** | **Yes (Full Editing Enabled)** | **Yes (Full Editing Enabled)** |

---

## 1. Stenography (Shorthand) Dictation & Transcription

* **Dictation Speed**: 80 Words Per Minute in Hindi for 5 minutes (Total 400 words).
* **Transcription Time**: Candidates are allotted standard transcription time on computer to convert their shorthand notes into formatted text.
* **Error Cap**: Total errors in shorthand transcription must strictly remain below 10%.

---

## 2. Computer Typing Evaluation & Interface

* **Bilingual Typing**: Candidates are tested on both Hindi (25 WPM) and English (30 WPM) typing proficiency.
* **Mangal Remington GAIL**: The Hindi test utilizes Unicode Mangal. Traditional KrutiDev typists can use Remington GAIL mapping seamlessly.
