The **Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSSB / RSMSSB) LDC / Junior Assistant** Phase-II examination consists of **100 marks** evaluated on computer-based typing speed and office efficiency.

---

## 1. Exam Structure & Marks Allocation

| Paper Section | Subject | Duration | Maximum Marks | Minimum Qualifying Marks |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Paper-I: Hindi** | Speed Test (DevLys 010) | 10 Minutes | 25 Marks | 9.0 Marks (36%) |
| | Efficiency Test (MS Word/Excel) | 10 Minutes | 25 Marks | 9.0 Marks (36%) |
| **Paper-II: English** | Speed Test | 10 Minutes | 25 Marks | 9.0 Marks (36%) |
| | Efficiency Test (MS Word/Excel) | 10 Minutes | 25 Marks | 9.0 Marks (36%) |
| **Total** | **Combined Phase-II** | **40 Minutes** | **100 Marks** | **36 Marks (Total)** |

---

## 2. Speed Calculation & Mark Slabs

To score the maximum 25 marks in the Speed Test:
* **Target Speed**: 50 WPM (Words Per Minute) in both Hindi and English.
* If speed falls below **28 WPM**, minimum qualifying marks (9/25) are not attained.

$$\text{Marks Awarded} = \left(\frac{\text{Net Speed Typed}}{50}\right) \times 25$$
