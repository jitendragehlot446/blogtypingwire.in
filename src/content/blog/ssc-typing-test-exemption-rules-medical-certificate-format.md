The **Staff Selection Commission (SSC)** provides specific statutory provisions granting **complete exemption from the Skill Test (DEST / Typing Test)** to eligible **Persons with Benchmark Disabilities (PwBD)** in examinations like **SSC CGL, CHSL, and Selection Posts**.

However, obtaining this exemption requires strict compliance with the **Commission's official Medical Certificate Annexure**.

---

## ⚡ Exemption Eligibility Criteria Matrix

| Disability Category | Permissible Impairment | Exemption Granted? | Required Documentation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Locomotor Disability (Both Arms)** | Permanent physical impairment of both upper limbs | **YES (Full Exemption)** | Annexure Certificate signed by CMO / Civil Surgeon |
| **Locomotor Disability (One Arm)** | Permanent physical limitation preventing fast typing | **YES (Full Exemption)** | Annexure Certificate signed by CMO / Civil Surgeon |
| **Visually Handicapped (VH)** | Complete blindness or severe low vision | **YES (Full Exemption)** | Disability Certificate from competent medical authority |
| **Hearing Handicapped (HH)** | Severe hearing impairment | **NO (Must appear in test)**| Hearing impairment does not impair manual typing |
| **Temporary Fractures / Injuries** | Plaster, sprains, recent limb trauma | **NO (Strictly not exempt)**| Exemption applies only to permanent benchmark conditions |

## 1. Official Medical Certificate Format (Annexure Guidelines)

To claim exemption from the typing test, eligible candidates must submit a formal medical certificate adhering to SSC's prescribed proforma:

### Key Requirements on the Certificate:
1. **Official Hospital Letterhead**: Must be issued on the stationery of a Government District Hospital.
2. **Specific Doctor Signatures**:
   * Must be signed by a specialist (Orthopedic Surgeon / Ophthalmologist).
   * **Must be countersigned by the Civil Surgeon / Chief Medical Officer (CMO)** of the district hospital with official seal and registration number.
3. **Explicit Medical Finding**: The certificate must explicitly certify:  
   *"...is permanently incapacitated for performing typewriting/data entry work due to physical disability..."*

> [!CAUTION]
> Certificates signed only by private clinics, MBBS medical officers, or AYUSH doctors are rejected summarily by SSC regional offices!

---

## 2. Procedure on the Day of the Examination

1. **Reporting to the Exam Center**: Even if you seek exemption, you must appear at your allotted Tier-II exam center on the scheduled examination day.
2. **Document Verification**: Present the original CMO-signed Medical Certificate along with your PwD Disability Certificate to the SSC Center Coordinator / Commission Official.
3. **System Exemption Entry**: The invigilator registers your exemption status on the server portal, marking your DEST status as *Exempted (EX)*.
