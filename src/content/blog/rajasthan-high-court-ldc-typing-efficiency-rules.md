The **Rajasthan High Court (RHC) LDC / Clerk Grade-II / Junior Judicial Assistant (JJA)** examination features one of the most competitive skill tests in India. Unlike standard SSC qualifying tests, the **100 marks** of the typing and efficiency test are directly added to your written exam score for the final merit list.

---

## 1. Complete 100-Mark Exam Pattern

The test is divided into **Paper-I (Speed Test)** and **Paper-II (Efficiency Test)**:

### 📑 Paper-I: Speed Test (50 Marks)
| Medium | Duration | Max Marks | Min Qualifying Marks (SC/ST/PwD) | Min Qualifying Marks (Others) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hindi Typing** (KrutiDev 010) | 5 Minutes | 25 Marks | 20 Marks (Combined) | 22.5 Marks (Combined) |
| **English Typing** | 5 Minutes | 25 Marks | - | - |

*Speed Formula for Full 25 Marks*:
$$\text{Required Speed for Full 25/25 Marks} = 50 \text{ WPM (8000 KDPH)}$$
$$\text{Minimum Speed for 11.25 Marks} = 26.67 \text{ WPM (Minimum 66.67 WPM equivalent)}$$

---

### 📊 Paper-II: Efficiency Test (50 Marks)
| Topic / Application | Duration | Max Marks | Min Qualifying (SC/ST) | Min Qualifying (Others) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **MS Word / Excel Formatting** | 10 Minutes | 50 Marks | 20 Marks | 22.5 Marks |

---

## 2. Key Formatting Tasks Asked in Efficiency Test
1. **Font & Size Operations**: Change font family, size to specific points (e.g. 14pt), italicize 3rd paragraph, strikethrough specific words.
2. **Paragraph Alignment & Spacing**: Left, Right, Center, Justified alignment, 1.15 / 1.5 line spacing, 6pt spacing before/after paragraph.
3. **Table Formatting**: Insert borders, highlight header row with specific background fill, sort table columns, merge cells.
4. **Header, Footer & Page Setup**: Set margins (Narrow/Moderate), add page numbers in specific format, insert watermark.

## 3. Recommended Preparation Strategy
* **KrutiDev 010 Mastery**: Practice Hindi typing on KrutiDev layout with special Alt codes (`Alt + 0161`, `Alt + 0216`, etc.) daily.
* **Accuracy over Speed**: Since speed tests are only 5 minutes long, even 2-3 mistakes can severely drop your marks.
