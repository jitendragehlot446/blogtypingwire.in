The **Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board (DSSSB)** conducts skill tests for **Lower Division Clerk (LDC), Junior Assistant (Service Dept / MCD), Stenographer, and Junior Judicial Assistant (JJA)**.

---

## 1. Exam Patterns & Speed Criteria

| Post Name | Medium | Duration | Key Depressions Target | Net Speed Required |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **LDC / Junior Assistant** | English | 10 Minutes | 2,100 Keystrokes | **35 WPM** |
| **LDC / Junior Assistant** | Hindi (Mangal/Kruti) | 10 Minutes | 1,800 Keystrokes | **30 WPM** |
| **Stenographer (Gr-III)** | English | 10 Minutes | 2,500 Keystrokes | **35 WPM** |
| **Stenographer (Gr-III)** | Hindi | 10 Minutes | 2,100 Keystrokes | **30 WPM** |
| **Delhi JJA (Courts)** | English | 10 Minutes | 2,500 Keystrokes | **35 WPM (Gross/Net)** |

---

## 2. Key DSSSB Instructions
* **Retyping Mandatory**: In DSSSB exams, if you finish the passage in 7-8 minutes, you should continue retyping from the start to accumulate maximum valid keystrokes.
