The **Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board (DSSSB)** conducts recruitment typing tests for **Junior Assistant, Lower Division Clerk (LDC), Stenographer, and Junior Judicial Assistant (JJA)** across Government of NCT of Delhi departments, Municipal Corporation of Delhi (MCD), and Delhi Jal Board.

## 1. DSSSB Speed Calculation & 5-Keystroke Standard

DSSSB adheres to the standard 5-keystroke definition standardized by the Directorate General of Employment and Training (DGET):

$$\text{Standard Words} = \frac{\text{Total Key Depressions Typed}}{5}$$

$$\text{Gross Speed (WPM)} = \frac{\text{Standard Words}}{\text{10 Minutes}}$$

$$\text{Net Speed (WPM)} = \text{Gross Speed} - \left( \frac{\text{Mistakes Counted}}{\text{10 Minutes}} \right)$$

### 🔁 The Retyping Rule Explained:
* If a candidate typing at 50 WPM completes a 350-word text in 7 minutes, they must **hit Space or Enter and continue typing the passage from the first word**.
* The computer continues tracking keystrokes up to minute 10:00. Ceasing to type after 7 minutes drops your 10-minute average speed and risks disqualification.

---

## 2. Mistake Penalties Breakdown

1. **Full Mistakes**: Every omitted word, substituted word, or added extraneous word.
2. **Half Mistakes**: Capitalization errors, punctuation omissions, and spacing irregularities.
3. Candidates exceeding the permissible error threshold fail the test regardless of their written Tier-I score.
