The **Official Notification of the Rajasthan High Court (Jodhpur)** for recruitment to **Junior Judicial Assistant (JJA), Junior Assistant (State/District Legal Services Authority), and Clerk Grade-II (District Courts)** establishes one of the highest-weightage skill tests in India.

The **100 marks** of the Typewriting & Efficiency Test directly determine final appointment and district allocation.

## 1. Speed Test Mathematical Marks Formula

Rajasthan High Court evaluates candidate speed based on exact Key Depressions:

`Required Speed for Full 25/25 Marks = 50 WPM (8,000 KDPH)`

`Minimum Speed for 11.25 Qualifying Marks = 26.67 WPM (4,267 KDPH)`

`Marks Awarded = (Net Key Depressions Typed / Target Keystrokes (8000/12)) × 25`

> [!CAUTION]
> If a candidate fails to achieve the minimum qualifying score of 22.5 marks in Paper-I or Paper-II, they are eliminated immediately, even if they scored 280/300 in the written exam!

## 2. Paper-II: 50-Mark Efficiency Test Tasks Breakdown

The Efficiency Test takes place in MS Word / Excel. Common tasks tested by the Rajasthan High Court examination board include:
1. **Paragraph Formatting**: Justifying paragraph 2, setting line spacing to 1.5, applying first-line indent of 0.5 inches.
2. **Font Styling**: Changing font size of selected text to 14pt, italicizing specific legal words, applying strikethrough to mistakes.
3. **Table Operations**: Merging specified table cells, highlighting header row with yellow fill, applying double borders.
4. **Header, Footer & Watermark**: Inserting page numbers in format *Page X of Y*, adding custom watermark *CONFIDENTIAL*.
