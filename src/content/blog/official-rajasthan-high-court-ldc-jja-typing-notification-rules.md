Under the **Rajasthan High Court Staff Service Rules** and official recruitment notifications for **Junior Judicial Assistant (JJA), Junior Assistant, and Clerk Grade-II**, the competitive merit is determined by combining written scores with **Section-B (Type-Writing Test on Computer - 100 Marks)**.

---

## 1. Official Notification Section-B Structure

### 📑 Paper-I: Speed Test (50 Marks)
| Subject / Language | Duration | Maximum Marks | Font Mandated by Notification |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hindi Speed Test** | **5 Minutes** | **25 Marks** | **KrutiDev 010** |
| **English Speed Test**| **5 Minutes** | **25 Marks** | **Times New Roman** |

$$\text{Full 25 Marks Criteria} = 50 \text{ WPM (8000 Key Depressions/Hour)}$$
$$\text{Minimum Qualifying Speed (11.25 Marks)} = 26.67 \text{ WPM}$$

---

### 📊 Paper-II: Efficiency Test (50 Marks)
| Subject | Duration | Maximum Marks | Pass Marks (SC/ST/PH) | Pass Marks (Others) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **MS Word / Excel Formatting** | **10 Minutes** | **50 Marks** | **20.0 Marks** | **22.5 Marks** |

---

## 2. Notification Efficiency Test Guidelines
* Candidates are given instructions to format text, paragraphs, tables, line spacing, margins, header/footer, and borders using Microsoft Word / Excel.
* Use of shortcut keys and menu formatting options is evaluated by automated test scripts.
