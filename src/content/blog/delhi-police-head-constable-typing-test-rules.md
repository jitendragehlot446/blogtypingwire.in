The **Delhi Police Head Constable (Ministerial)** examination (conducted by SSC) awards up to **25 marks** in the final merit list based directly on your typing speed achieved during the 10-minute test.

---

## 1. Speed vs Marks Allocation Slab (Out of 25 Marks)

| English Typing Speed (WPM) | Hindi Typing Speed (WPM) | Marks Awarded (Out of 25) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Above 50 WPM** | **Above 45 WPM** | **25 Marks (Maximum)** |
| **46 - 50 WPM** | **41 - 45 WPM** | **21 Marks** |
| **41 - 45 WPM** | **36 - 40 WPM** | **18 Marks** |
| **36 - 40 WPM** | **31 - 35 WPM** | **15 Marks** |
| **30 - 35 WPM** | **25 - 30 WPM** | **10 Marks (Minimum Qualifying)** |
| **Below 30 WPM** | **Below 25 WPM** | **Disqualified (0 Marks)** |
