The **Indo-Tibetan Border Police (ITBP)** recruits **Head Constables (Ministerial - Direct & Departmental)** to handle clerical administration, communication, and personnel records at high-altitude border posts and command establishments.

Shortlisted candidates must clear the computer typing test. Below are the official rules, speed benchmarks, and evaluation guidelines.

---

## ⚡ Quick Exam Snapshot (TL;DR Fact Matrix)

| Parameter | English Typing Standard | Hindi Typing Standard |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Minimum Required Speed** | **35 Words Per Minute (WPM)** | **30 Words Per Minute (WPM)** |
| **Key Depressions (KDPH)** | **10,500 KDPH on Computer** | **9,000 KDPH on Computer** |
| **Test Duration** | **10 Minutes** | **10 Minutes** |
| **Passage Volume** | **Approx. 350 Words (1,750 Keystrokes)** | **Approx. 300 Words (1,500 Keystrokes)** |
| **Mandatory Font** | Standard English Font | Mangal Unicode (Inscript / Remington GAIL) |
| **Permissible Error Limit** | **Maximum 10.5% Mistakes Allowed** | **Maximum 10.5% Mistakes Allowed** |
| **Test Nature** | **Qualifying Only (Mandatory)** | **Qualifying Only (Mandatory)** |
| **Backspace Permitted** | **Yes (Full Editing Enabled)** | **Yes (Full Editing Enabled)** |

---

## 1. Speed Evaluation & Mistake Formula

* **Standard Word**: 5 key depressions = 1 word.
* **Speed Formula**:
  `Net Speed (WPM) = (Correct Keystrokes / 5 / 10 Minutes)`
* Candidates who type the entire passage with a steady pace and correct capitalization pass easily.
