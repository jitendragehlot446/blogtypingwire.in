The **Bihar Vidhan Sabha (Legislative Assembly) Secretariat** and **Bihar Vidhan Parishad (Legislative Council) Secretariat** recruit **Data Entry Operators (DEO), Reporters (Prativedak), and Lower Division Clerks (LDC)**.

Working in parliamentary secretariats requires high typing accuracy for real-time legislative proceedings and gazette documentation. Below are the official rules and guidelines.

---

## ⚡ Quick Exam Snapshot (TL;DR Fact Matrix)

| Parameter | Official Bihar Sachivalaya Standard |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Recruitment Posts** | Data Entry Operator (DEO) & Assistant / LDC |
| **Required Speed (Hindi)** | **30 Words Per Minute (WPM)** in 10 Minutes (300 Words) |
| **Required Speed (English)** | **30 Words Per Minute (WPM)** in 10 Minutes (300 Words) |
| **Mandatory Hindi Font** | **Mangal Unicode (Remington GAIL Layout)** |
| **Permissible Error Limit** | **Maximum 5% Errors Allowed** |
| **Computer Proficiency Test** | **MS Word, Excel, PowerPoint & Internet (Qualifying)** |
| **Test Nature** | **Bilingual Qualification** |
| **Backspace Permitted** | **Yes (Full Editing Enabled)** |

---

## 1. Bilingual Typing & Font Standards

* Candidates must qualify **both Hindi and English** typing sessions.
* The Hindi test strictly uses **Mangal Remington GAIL**, identical to Beltron and BSSC exam standards.
