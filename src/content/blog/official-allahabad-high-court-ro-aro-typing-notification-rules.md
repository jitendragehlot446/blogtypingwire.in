The **High Court of Judicature at Allahabad (AHC)** conducts the **Computer Knowledge Test / Typing Test** as Part-II of the combined recruitment examination for **Review Officer (RO), Assistant Review Officer (ARO), and Computer Assistant**.

---

## 1. Official Notification Clause Breakdown (Part-II Exam)

| Notification Parameter | Official Allahabad High Court Specification |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Exam Component** | Part-II (Computer Knowledge & Typing Test) |
| **Medium of Test** | **English (Mandatory)** |
| **Duration of Session** | **15 to 20 Minutes** |
| **Master Passage Length** | **Approximately 500 Words (Legal & Official Text)** |
| **Minimum Required Speed** | **25 WPM (Words Per Minute)** |
| **Maximum Marks** | **50 Marks** |
| **Minimum Qualifying Marks** | **25 Marks (50% Strict Cutoff)** |
| **Evaluation Method** | Word-by-Word accuracy matching |

---

## 2. Official Evaluation & Marking Formula

$$\text{Raw Score} = \left(\frac{\text{Correct Words Typed}}{500}\right) \times 50$$

$$\text{Net Score (Out of 50)} = \text{Raw Score} - (\text{Total Mistakes} \times 0.10)$$

* **Disqualification Rule**: If Net Score is **less than 25.00 marks**, the candidate is eliminated regardless of high marks in Part-I written paper.
