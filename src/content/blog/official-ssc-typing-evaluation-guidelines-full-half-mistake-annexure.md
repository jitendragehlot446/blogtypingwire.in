The **Staff Selection Commission (SSC)** evaluates typing tests and Data Entry Speed Tests across Tier-II examinations using the official **Evaluation of Typing Test / DEST / Skill Test Guidelines**.

---

## 1. Official Evaluation Annexure Breakdown

| Error Category | Nature of Error Committed by Candidate | Penalty Weightage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Full Mistake** | **Omission**: Missing any word or number from master passage | **1.0 (100% Value)** |
| **Full Mistake** | **Substitution**: Replacing a master word with an incorrect word | **1.0 (100% Value)** |
| **Full Mistake** | **Addition**: Inserting unnecessary words/figures | **1.0 (100% Value)** |
| **Full Mistake** | **Spelling Mistake**: Any typo in letters within a word | **1.0 (100% Value)** |
| **Half Mistake** | **Spacing Error**: Combining two words without space or double spaces | **0.5 (50% Value)** |
| **Half Mistake** | **Punctuation Error**: Omitting or wrongly placing periods, commas | **0.5 (50% Value)** |
| **Half Mistake** | **Capitalization Error**: Typing lower-case for proper nouns or vice-versa | **0.5 (50% Value)** |

---

## 2. Official Evaluation Formula

$$\text{Total Errors} = \text{Count of Full Mistakes} + \left(\frac{\text{Count of Half Mistakes}}{2}\right)$$

$$\text{Error Percentage (\%)} = \left(\frac{\text{Total Errors} \times 100}{\text{Total Words Typed in Master Text}}\right)$$

### 🎯 Official Category Error Cutoffs:
* **SSC CGL DEST (Tax Assistant / Executive Assistant)**: UR **5%**, OBC/EWS **7%**, SC/ST **7%**.
* **SSC CHSL (LDC / JSA)**: UR **7%**, OBC/EWS/SC/ST/PwD **10%**.
* **SSC CHSL (DEO Grade A)**: UR **5%**, Reserved Categories **7%**.
