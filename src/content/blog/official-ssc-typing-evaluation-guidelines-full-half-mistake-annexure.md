## 1. Official Definitions: Full Mistakes vs Half Mistakes

### 🔴 Full Mistakes (1.0 Penalty Each)
1. **Omission of Word**: Skipping any word, number, or figure printed in the master passage.
2. **Substitution of Word**: Replacing a correct master word with a completely different word (e.g., typing *president* instead of *parliament*).
3. **Addition of Word**: Inserting unprinted words, phrases, or extra lines into the submitted transcript.

### 🟡 Half Mistakes (0.5 Penalty Each)
1. **Spacing Errors**:
   * Omitting space between two words (e.g., *courtorder* instead of *court order*).
   * Inserting unwanted extra spaces within a word (e.g., *co urt*).
2. **Punctuation Errors**:
   * Missing commas, periods, quotation marks, or semicolons.
   * Inserting incorrect punctuation marks.
3. **Capitalization Errors**:
   * Typing lowercase letters where capital letters are required at sentence beginnings or proper nouns (*india* instead of *India*).
   * Typing unwanted capital letters mid-word (*pRoject*).
4. **Spelling Inaccuracies**:
   * Transposition of letters within a word (*teh* instead of *the*).
   * Minor typographical errors resulting in misspelled words.

## 2. Official SSC Error Percentage Calculation Formula

`Total Errors = Full Mistakes + (Half Mistakes × 0.5)`

`Percentage Error (%) = ( (Total Errors / Total Words in Master Passage) ) × 100`

### 📊 Practical Numerical Example:
* Master Passage contains **400 words**.
* Candidate commits **12 Full Mistakes** (Omissions/Substitutions).
* Candidate commits **8 Half Mistakes** (Punctuation/Capitalization).
* Total Mistakes = 12 + (8 × 0.5) = 12 + 4 = 16 Errors.
* Percentage Error = \left(\frac{16}{400}\right) × 100 = 4.0%.
* **Result**: Since 4.0% is below the 5% UR threshold, the candidate **QUALIFIES with distinction**!
