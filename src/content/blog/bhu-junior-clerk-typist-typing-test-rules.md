**Banaras Hindu University (BHU)**, an Institute of National Importance located in Varanasi, recruits **Junior Clerks and Office Assistants** across administrative faculties and university departments through the **National Testing Agency (NTA)**.

Shortlisted candidates must clear the computer typing test. Below are the official rules and guidelines.

---

## ⚡ Quick Exam Snapshot (TL;DR Fact Matrix)

| Parameter | English Typing Medium | Hindi Typing Medium |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Minimum Required Speed** | **30 Words Per Minute (WPM)** | **25 Words Per Minute (WPM)** |
| **Key Depressions Equivalent** | **1,500 Keystrokes in 10 Min** | **1,250 Keystrokes in 10 Min** |
| **Test Duration** | **10 Minutes** | **10 Minutes** |
| **Testing Agency** | **National Testing Agency (NTA)** | **National Testing Agency (NTA)** |
| **Font Standard** | Standard English Font | Mangal Unicode (Inscript / Remington GAIL) |
| **Nature of Test** | **Qualifying Only** | **Qualifying Only** |
| **Backspace Permitted** | **Yes (Full Editing Enabled)** | **Yes (Full Editing Enabled)** |

---

## 1. NTA Testing Interface & Evaluation

* **Passage Display**: Dual-box testing interface with continuous paragraph text.
* **Accuracy Requirement**: Minimum 90% accuracy is recommended to ensure error deductions do not drop your net speed below the 30/25 WPM benchmarks.
