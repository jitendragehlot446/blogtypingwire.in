Every state government recruitment board conducts clerical and **Lower Division Clerk (LDC)** skill tests with distinct scoring weightage and font requirements as prescribed in their respective state service rules.

---

## 1. Official Notification Comparison Matrix

| State / Board | Exam Name | Duration | Hindi Requirement | English Requirement | Test Nature |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Rajasthan (RSSB)** | LDC / Junior Assistant | 10 Min Speed + 10 Min Efficiency | 50 WPM (DevLys 010) [25+25 Marks] | 50 WPM [25+25 Marks] | **100 Marks Added to Merit** |
| **Rajasthan (High Court)** | RHC LDC / JJA | 5 Min Speed + 10 Min Efficiency | 50 WPM (KrutiDev 010) [25 Marks] | 50 WPM [25 Marks] | **100 Marks Added to Merit** |
| **Uttar Pradesh (UPSSSC)** | Junior Assistant / Clerk | 5 Min (Hindi) + 5 Min (English) | 25 WPM (Mangal Inscript/Gail) | 30 WPM | **Qualifying (Both Mandatory)** |
| **Bihar (BSSC)** | 2nd Inter Level Clerk | 10 Minutes | 30 WPM (Mangal Font) | 35 WPM | **Qualifying** |
| **Bihar (Beltron)** | Data Entry Operator | 10 Minutes | 25 WPM (Remington Gail) | 30 WPM | **Qualifying / Merit** |
| **Madhya Pradesh (MP HC)** | Assistant Grade-3 (AG-3) | 10 Minutes | 30 WPM (KrutiDev/Mangal) | 35 WPM | **Merit Scoring** |

---

## 2. Font & Layout Requirements by State
* **Rajasthan**: Strictly **KrutiDev 010 / DevLys 010** (Legacy Non-Unicode Remington).
* **Uttar Pradesh**: **Unicode Mangal Font** (Inscript / Remington Gail).
* **Bihar**: **Unicode Mangal Font** (Remington Gail).
