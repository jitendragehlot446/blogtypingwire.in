Typing aspirants across India often face confusion because different state recruiting agencies follow drastically different rules for **Lower Division Clerk (LDC), Junior Assistant, and Clerk-Typist** positions.

This comprehensive guide presents a direct comparative matrix across **Rajasthan (RSSB & High Court), Uttar Pradesh (UPSSSC & High Court), Bihar (BSSC & Civil Court), and Central exams**.

## 1. Key Font & Keyboard Differences

### 1. Legacy Non-Unicode (Rajasthan Standard)
* **Fonts**: KrutiDev 010, DevLys 010.
* **Layout**: Remington Typewriter.
* **Characteristics**: Requires memorization of numeric Alt codes for half-letters, conjuncts, and vowel matras.

### 2. Unicode Mangal (UP, Bihar, SSC & Central Standard)
* **Fonts**: Mangal, Nirmala UI, Arial Unicode.
* **Layouts**: Inscript (Standardized by GoI) or Remington (Gail).
* **Characteristics**: Unicode compliant; conjuncts are created organically using the Halant key without memorizing ASCII codes.

---

## 2. Merit-Scored vs Qualifying Examinations

* **Merit-Scored Skill Tests**: In Rajasthan (RSSB & High Court) and Allahabad High Court, scoring high marks in typing directly compensates for lower marks in written examinations. An aspirant with 48/50 in typing often overtakes candidates with higher written test scores.
* **Qualifying Skill Tests**: In SSC, UPSSSC, and BSSC, speed and accuracy above the cutoff guarantee qualification, but extra speed beyond the cutoff does not yield extra marks.
