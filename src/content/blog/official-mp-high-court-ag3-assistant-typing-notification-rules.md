Under the official **Madhya Pradesh High Court Service Rules**, the recruitment of **Assistant Grade-3 (AG-3), Stenographer Grade-2/3, and Junior Judicial Assistant** features a competitive **100-Mark Main Examination** where typing accuracy directly determines candidate rank.

---

## 1. Official Notification Main Exam Breakdown (100 Marks)

| Subject / Paper | Master Word Count | Duration | Maximum Marks | Font / Software |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hindi Typing Test** | **Approx 300 Words** | **10 Minutes** | **30 Marks** | **KrutiDev 010 / Mangal** |
| **English Typing Test** | **Approx 350 Words** | **10 Minutes** | **30 Marks** | **Standard QWERTY** |
| **Computer Knowledge & English**| Multiple Choice & Formatting | **20 Minutes** | **40 Marks** | Objective Paper |
| **Total Merit Score** | - | **40 Minutes** | **100 Marks** | **Combined Merit** |

---

## 2. Notification Deduction Rules
* **Spelling Mistake / Omission / Substitution**: **0.50 Mark Deducted per error**.
* **Spacing / Punctuation Error**: **0.10 Mark Deducted per error**.
