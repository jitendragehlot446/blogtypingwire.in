The **Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) Centralised Recruitment Board (ICRB)** recruits **Assistants and Junior Personal Assistants (JPA)** for India's prestigious space missions, laboratories, and rocket launching centres.

The computer literacy and typing test is the final hurdle for shortlisted candidates. Below are the official rules and guidelines.

---

## ⚡ Quick Exam Snapshot (TL;DR Fact Matrix)

| Parameter | Official ISRO ICRB Standard |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Recruitment Post** | Assistant & Junior Personal Assistant (JPA) |
| **Mandatory Medium** | **English on Computer** |
| **Required Speed Benchmark** | **35 Words Per Minute (WPM)** |
| **Key Depressions (KDPH)** | **10,500 KDPH on Computer** |
| **Test Duration** | **10 Minutes** |
| **Required Keystrokes** | **Approx. 1,750 Keystrokes in 10 Min** |
| **Skill Test Qualifying Cutoff** | **Minimum 60% Marks in Skill Test (50% for Reserved)** |
| **Test Nature** | **Qualifying Only** |
| **Backspace Permitted** | **Yes (Full Editing Enabled)** |

---

## 1. Skill Test Evaluation & Format

* **Word Standard**: 5 key depressions per word.
* **Computer Proficiency**: In addition to typing, the skill test may include practical questions on MS Word, MS Excel formatting, and PowerPoint slide preparation.
