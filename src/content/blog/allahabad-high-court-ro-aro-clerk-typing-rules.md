The **High Court of Judicature at Allahabad (Allahabad High Court)** conducts recruitment for **Review Officer (RO), Assistant Review Officer (ARO), Computer Assistant, and Group-C Clerical posts**.

Unlike central examinations where typing is merely qualifying, in Allahabad High Court the **Typing Test carries 50 marks** that are directly added to your written exam total. Scoring 48+ out of 50 in typing is often the deciding factor in securing top state ranks.

## 1. Official 50-Mark Evaluation & Penalty Formula

The NTA evaluates candidate transcripts based on character-level precision. Full marks (50/50) require completing all 500 words with zero mistakes within 20 minutes.

### 🔴 Error Penalty Deduction Rules
* **Each Full Mistake (Omission/Substitution)**: Deducts **0.1 marks** from the total 50 marks.
* **Each Half Mistake (Spelling/Punctuation/Spacing)**: Deducts **0.05 marks**.
* **Incomplete Typing Penalty**: If a candidate types fewer than 500 words, marks are calculated proportionally:

$$\text{Marks Obtained} = \left( \frac{\text{Correct Words Typed}}{500} \times 50 \right) - \text{Total Mistake Deductions}$$

> [!IMPORTANT]
> To qualify, candidates MUST achieve both **minimum 25 WPM speed** AND **minimum 25 out of 50 marks**. If your speed is 24.9 WPM, you are automatically failed.

## 2. NTA Typing Test Interface & Software Behavior

The examination is administered on the NTA browser-based testing portal:
1. **Split-Screen UI**: Master passage appears on the upper half of the monitor; candidate input field is situated on the lower half.
2. **Text Highlighting**: The currently active word is highlighted in color, assisting visual tracking.
3. **Word Wrap & Auto-Scroll**: As lines are completed, the text automatically scrolls down to maintain focus.
4. **Countdown Timer**: A persistent live clock displays remaining minutes and seconds in the top-right corner.

## 3. Proven Strategies to Score 48+ Out of 50 Marks

1. **Prioritize 100% Accuracy Over 50 WPM Speed**: Since speed requirement is only 25 WPM, typing calmly at 35 WPM with 99% accuracy will yield 49/50 marks. Rushing at 60 WPM with 15 mistakes will drop your score down to 35/50 marks.
2. **Practice on Standard QWERTY Desktop Keyboards**: Never practice on low-profile laptop keyboards. High-court exam centers feature standard mechanical/membrane USB keyboards with deep key travel.
3. **Simulate 20-Minute Sustained Sessions**: Most candidates experience finger fatigue between minute 12 and 18. Practice dedicated 20-minute sessions on [TypingWire.in](https://typingwire.in).
