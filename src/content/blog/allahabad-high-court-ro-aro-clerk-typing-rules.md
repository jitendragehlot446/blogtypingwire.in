The **Allahabad High Court (AHC)** conducts computer-based typing tests for recruitment to **Review Officer (RO), Assistant Review Officer (ARO), Computer Assistant, and Group 'C' Clerk** posts.

---

## 1. Allahabad High Court Typing Test Overview

| Criteria | Specification |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Medium** | English (Mandatory) |
| **Duration** | 15 Minutes |
| **Maximum Marks** | **50 Marks** |
| **Minimum Qualifying Marks** | **25 Marks (50%)** |
| **Passage Length** | Approx. 500 Words |
| **Minimum Speed Required** | **25 WPM** |

---

## 2. Marking Scheme & Deduction Rules

Candidates start with a base score derived from their completed word count and accuracy:

$$\text{Marks Awarded} = \left(\frac{\text{Correct Words Typed}}{500}\right) \times 50 - (\text{Mistake Penalties})$$

* Every spelling error, omission, or substitution carries a negative penalty (typically 0.1 mark per mistake).
* If a candidate types less than 500 words or commits excessive errors, their score drops below the mandatory 25-mark cutoff, resulting in elimination.
