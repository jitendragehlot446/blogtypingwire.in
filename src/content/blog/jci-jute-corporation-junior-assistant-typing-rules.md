The **Jute Corporation of India Limited (JCI)** is a premier Central Public Sector Undertaking (CPSU) under the Ministry of Textiles. JCI recruits **Junior Assistants** to oversee operational accounting, procurement management, and regional administration.

The computer typing test is a mandatory qualifying requirement for candidates shortlisted through the CBT. Below are the official rules and guidelines.

---

## ⚡ Quick Exam Snapshot (TL;DR Fact Matrix)

| Parameter | English Typing Standard | Hindi Typing Standard |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Minimum Required Speed** | **35 Words Per Minute (WPM)** | **30 Words Per Minute (WPM)** |
| **Key Depressions (KDPH)** | **10,500 KDPH on Computer** | **9,000 KDPH on Computer** |
| **Test Duration** | **10 Minutes** | **10 Minutes** |
| **Passage Volume** | **Approx. 350 Words (1,750 Keystrokes)** | **Approx. 300 Words (1,500 Keystrokes)** |
| **Mandatory Font** | Standard English Font | Mangal Unicode (Inscript / Remington GAIL) |
| **Permissible Error Limit** | **Maximum 5% to 7% Errors** | **Maximum 5% to 7% Errors** |
| **Test Nature** | **Qualifying Only** | **Qualifying Only** |
| **Backspace Permitted** | **Yes (Full Editing Enabled)** | **Yes (Full Editing Enabled)** |

---

## 1. Official Speed Calculation

* **Word Standard**: 5 key depressions = 1 word.
* **Speed Formula**:
  `Net WPM = (Gross Keystrokes - (Mistake Penalty × 5) / 5 × 10 Minutes)`
* Candidates maintaining at least 95% accuracy easily clear the qualifying net WPM threshold.
