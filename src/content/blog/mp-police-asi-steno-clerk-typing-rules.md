The **Madhya Pradesh Police Headquarters (PHQ Bhopal) / MP Employees Selection Board (MPESB)** recruits ministerial staff including **Assistant Sub-Inspector (ASI - Clerk / Ministerial / Accounts)** and **Subedar (Stenographer)**.

Given the administrative nature of police record-keeping and FIR management on the CCTNS portal, computer typing proficiency is a core qualification. Below are the official rules and guidelines.

## 1. MP CPCT Scorecard Integration

Unlike other state police forces that conduct their own standalone typing test during physical dates, MP Police primarily accepts candidates who have already qualified the **MP CPCT (Computer Proficiency Certification Test)**:
* **Hindi Typing Qualification**: Minimum 20 WPM (50% marks) or 30+ WPM (60%+ marks) on Remington GAIL.
* **Computer Theory Section**: Minimum 50% qualifying score (38 marks out of 75).
* **Scorecard Validity**: Scorecard must be valid as of the closing date of the application.

---

## 2. Practical Typing Test Guidelines (Where Applicable)

When MP Police conducts a direct departmental or special typing skill test:
* Candidates are given a standard Hindi passage of 300 to 350 words.
* Punctuation, paragraph breaks, and accuracy are assessed on a negative marking matrix.
