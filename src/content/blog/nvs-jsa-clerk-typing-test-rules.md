The **Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti (NVS)** recruits **Junior Secretariat Assistants (JSA - HQ/RO Cadre & JNV Cadre)** through a nationwide computer-based recruitment drive.

Following the computer-based written test (CBT), shortlisted candidates must appear for the mandatory **Skill Test / Typing Test**. Below is the official breakdown of NVS typing rules and speed criteria.

---

## ⚡ Quick Exam Snapshot (TL;DR Fact Matrix)

| Parameter | English Typing Medium | Hindi Typing Medium |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Minimum Required Speed** | **35 Words Per Minute (WPM)** | **30 Words Per Minute (WPM)** |
| **Key Depressions (KDPH)** | **10,500 KDPH on Computer** | **9,000 KDPH on Computer** |
| **Test Duration** | **10 Minutes** | **10 Minutes** |
| **Passage Volume** | **Approx. 350 Words (1,750 Keystrokes)** | **Approx. 300 Words (1,500 Keystrokes)** |
| **Font Standard** | Standard English Font | Mangal Font (Inscript / Remington GAIL) |
| **Evaluation Method** | Net Speed after Error Deduction | Net Speed after Error Deduction |
| **Test Nature** | **Qualifying Only** | **Qualifying Only** |
| **Backspace Permitted** | **Yes (Full Editing Enabled)** | **Yes (Full Editing Enabled)** |

---

## 1. Speed Calculation & Error Criteria

* **Word Formula**: In alignment with central government recruitment standards, 1 standard word equals **5 characters / key depressions**.
* **Error Deduction**:
  `Net WPM = (Gross Keystrokes - (Mistakes × 5) / 5 × 10 Minutes)`
  Candidates must maintain high accuracy to prevent net speed deductions from pulling their final score below the mandatory 35 WPM (English) or 30 WPM (Hindi) thresholds.

---

## 2. Examination Hall Environment & Software Features

* **CBSE / NTA Testing Platform**: The interface features a dual-pane screen layout where the master text appears in the upper window and candidate input is typed into the lower scrollable box.
* **Scroll Bar**: If the passage exceeds the viewport, candidates can scroll using mouse wheel or keyboard arrow keys.
* **Practice Strategy**: Practice on full-length 10-minute drills with standard membrane keyboards to adapt to test-center typing conditions.
