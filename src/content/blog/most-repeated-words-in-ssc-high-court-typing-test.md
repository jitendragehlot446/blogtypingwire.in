## 5. Deliberate 15-Minute Daily Drill Routine

To maximize muscle memory retention from this word list:

1. **Round 1 (5 Minutes - Rhythmic Accuracy)**:
   - Type the words slowly at a constant tempo (1 keystroke per second).
   - Do **not** rush. Ensure zero typos. If you make a mistake, retype that word 5 times cleanly.
2. **Round 2 (5 Minutes - Speed Bursting)**:
   - Type the words in bursts of 5 words at maximum finger speed.
   - Rest your hands for 10 seconds between bursts.
3. **Round 3 (5 Minutes - Full Paragraph Integration)**:
