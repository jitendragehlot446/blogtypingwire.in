The **Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board (DSSSB)** recruits **Junior Judicial Assistants (JJA)** for the **District & Sessions Courts of Delhi**.

Given the strict accuracy demands of judicial court registries, legal proceedings, and case filing, the typing test has high accuracy standards. Below are the official rules and guidelines.

---

## ⚡ Quick Exam Snapshot (TL;DR Fact Matrix)

| Parameter | Official Delhi District Court JJA Standard |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Recruitment Post** | Junior Judicial Assistant (JJA - Delhi District Courts) |
| **Mandatory Medium** | **English on Computer (35 WPM) / Hindi (30 WPM where applicable)** |
| **Required Speed Benchmark** | **35 Words Per Minute (WPM)** |
| **Test Duration** | **10 Minutes** |
| **Required Keystrokes** | **Approx. 1,750 Keystrokes in 10 Min** |
| **Strict Accuracy Cutoff** | **Maximum 3% Permissible Errors Allowed** |
| **Passage Theme** | Legal judgments, petitions, court orders, statutes |
| **Test Nature** | **Qualifying Phase (Mandatory to Clear)** |
| **Backspace Permitted** | **Yes (Full Editing Enabled)** |

---

## 1. Legal Passage Characteristics & 3% Cutoff

* **Judicial Context**: Passages contain legal citations (e.g., `Section 302 IPC`, `AIR 1994 SC`), judicial case names, and technical legal terms.
* **Accuracy Priority**: Candidates must keep total errors strictly within **3%**. At 350 words typed, no more than 10 total mistake points are tolerated.
