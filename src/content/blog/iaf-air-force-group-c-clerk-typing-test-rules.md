The **Indian Air Force (IAF)** recruits civilian personnel for **Group 'C' Lower Division Clerk (LDC) and Typist posts** across various Air Force Stations, Depots, and Commands.

The skill test on computer is a decisive qualifying phase for candidates shortlisted through the written examination. Here are the official rules, speed benchmarks, and evaluation criteria based on IAF recruitment notifications.

---

## ⚡ Quick Exam Snapshot (TL;DR Fact Matrix)

| Parameter | English Typing Medium | Hindi Typing Medium |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Minimum Required Speed** | **35 Words Per Minute (WPM)** | **30 Words Per Minute (WPM)** |

## 1. Official Speed Calculation & Formula

According to the Ministry of Defence rules for civilian posts:
* **English Typists**:
  `Net Speed (WPM) = (Correct Keystrokes / 5 / 10 Minutes)`
  Candidates must type at least 1,750 correct keystrokes in 10 minutes (35 WPM).
* **Hindi Typists**:
  `Net Speed (WPM) = (Correct Keystrokes / 5 / 10 Minutes)`
  Candidates must type at least 1,500 correct keystrokes in 10 minutes (30 WPM).

## 2. Test Center Atmosphere & Practical Ground Reality

IAF Group C skill tests are conducted inside designated Air Force Base computer labs:
* **Keyboard Variety**: Test centers use desktop systems with standard membrane keyboards (such as HP, Dell, or Lenovo). 
* **Hard Copy vs Screen Typing**: Depending on the specific station command, passages may be provided either on-screen or as printed physical sheets placed beside the keyboard.
* **Accuracy Priority**: Ensure minimal spelling and punctuation mistakes; even minor omissions can drop your net WPM below the 35/30 threshold.
