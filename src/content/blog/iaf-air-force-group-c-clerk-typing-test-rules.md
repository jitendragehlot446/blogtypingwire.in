The **Indian Air Force (IAF)** recruits civilian personnel for **Group 'C' Lower Division Clerk (LDC) and Typist posts** across various Air Force Stations, Depots, and Commands.

The skill test on computer is a decisive qualifying phase for candidates shortlisted through the written examination. Here are the official rules, speed benchmarks, and evaluation criteria based on IAF recruitment notifications.

## 1. Official Speed Calculation & Formula

According to the Ministry of Defence rules for civilian posts:
* **English Typists**:
  `Net Speed (WPM) = (Correct Keystrokes / 5 / 10 Minutes)`
  Candidates must type at least 1,750 correct keystrokes in 10 minutes (35 WPM).
* **Hindi Typists**:
  `Net Speed (WPM) = (Correct Keystrokes / 5 / 10 Minutes)`
  Candidates must type at least 1,500 correct keystrokes in 10 minutes (30 WPM).

## 2. Test Center Atmosphere & Practical Ground Reality

IAF Group C skill tests are conducted inside designated Air Force Base computer labs:
* **Keyboard Variety**: Test centers use desktop systems with standard membrane keyboards (such as HP, Dell, or Lenovo). 
* **Hard Copy vs Screen Typing**: Depending on the specific station command, passages may be provided either on-screen or as printed physical sheets placed beside the keyboard.
* **Accuracy Priority**: Ensure minimal spelling and punctuation mistakes; even minor omissions can drop your net WPM below the 35/30 threshold.

## Worked Net Speed & Error Calculation Example

Understanding how IAF Air Force Group C LDC converts raw keystrokes into final qualifying speed prevents last-minute surprises:

* **Step 1 — Gross Words**: Divide total keystrokes (including spaces) by 5. Suppose in **10 minutes**, you type **1875 keystrokes** (375 gross words).
* **Step 2 — Mistake Penalty**: Suppose you commit **10 total mistakes** (each full mistake = 1 word; each half mistake = 0.5 word).
* **Step 3 — Net Speed**: Subtract mistakes from gross words (or apply the board's penalty rule) and divide by test duration. Your net qualifying speed comes to approximately **36.5 WPM**, keeping you safely above the **35 WPM English / 30 WPM Hindi** cutoff.

## Common Disqualification Traps & Exam Hall Checklist

1. **Primary Disqualification Risk**: Defense rally / Air Force station keyboards are often stiff membrane units requiring firm keystrokes.
2. **Font & Layout Verification (Mangal Unicode & Standard QWERTY)**: Always verify your selected keyboard layout during the 2-minute mock warm-up before the live timer starts.
3. **Backspace Discipline**: While backspace is permitted, retyping an entire sentence wastes 8–10 seconds. Fix immediate typos within 1–2 characters and keep moving forward.
4. **Line-Skip Prevention**: Keep your eyes 1–2 words ahead of your fingers on the upper passage pane so auto-scrolling never causes a skipped line.
5. **Hardware Readiness**: Test centers typically provide standard USB membrane keyboards (Dell/HP/TVS). Practice at least 50% of your drills on a standard membrane keyboard to build finger stamina.
