When typing for hours every day, the right mechanical switches make a tremendous difference in speed, precision, and hand comfort.

---

## 1. The Three Switch Categories

### 🔵 Clicky Switches (e.g. Cherry MX Blue, Kailh Box White)
* **Feel**: Pronounced tactile bump + audible click sound.
* **Actuation Force**: 50g - 60g.
* **Best For**: Typists who love acoustic and sensory confirmation for every keystroke. Not recommended in quiet offices.

### 🟤 Tactile Switches (e.g. Cherry MX Brown, Gateron Brown, Holy Panda)
* **Feel**: Smooth keystroke with a subtle tactile bump at the actuation point, without loud clicks.
* **Actuation Force**: 45g - 55g.
* **Best For**: The all-around champion for writers, programmers, and speed typists.

### 🔴 Linear Switches (e.g. Cherry MX Red, Gateron Yellow, Speed Silver)
* **Feel**: Completely smooth from top to bottom with zero bump.
* **Actuation Force**: 45g.
* **Best For**: Fast gaming and rapid burst typing with minimal finger strain.

---

## 2. Quick Comparison

| Switch Color | Feel | Sound Level | Best Use Case |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Red** | Linear (Smooth) | Quiet | Gaming & High-speed bursts |
| **Brown** | Tactile (Bump) | Medium | General Typing & Daily Work |
| **Blue** | Clicky (Bump + Click) | Loud | Satisfying retro typing feel |

---

## Summary
For competitive typing and exam preparation, **Tactile Brown** or smooth **Linear Red/Yellow** switches offer the optimal balance of speed and comfort.
