The **Bihar Rural Livelihoods Promotion Society (BRLPS - JEEViKA)** recruits professionals, Community Coordinators (CC), Office Assistants, and **Data Entry Operators (DEO)** across district and block project management units in Bihar.

For posts involving administrative and office data processing, the computer typing test is an essential qualifying gate. Below are the official rules, speed criteria, and font guidelines.

---

## ⚡ Quick Exam Snapshot (TL;DR Fact Matrix)

| Parameter | English Typing Medium | Hindi Typing Medium |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Required Speed** | **30 Words Per Minute (WPM)** | **25 Words Per Minute (WPM)** |
| **Key Depressions Equivalent** | **1,500 KD in 10 Minutes** | **1,250 KD in 10 Minutes** |
| **Duration of Test** | **5 to 10 Minutes** | **5 to 10 Minutes** |
| **Font Standard** | Standard English Font | Mangal Unicode (Remington GAIL) |
| **Accuracy Cutoff** | **Minimum 90% Net Accuracy** | **Minimum 85% Net Accuracy** |
| **Test Nature** | **Qualifying Only** | **Qualifying Only** |
| **Backspace Permitted** | **Yes (Full Editing Enabled)** | **Yes (Full Editing Enabled)** |

## 1. Hindi Typing Font & Key Layout (Mangal Remington GAIL)

* In alignment with **Bihar Government Department of General Administration (GAD)** standards (used in Beltron and BSSC), Bihar Jeevika utilizes the **Mangal Unicode font** on the **Remington GAIL** keyboard layout.
* Candidates trained on KrutiDev typewriters can seamlessly adapt to Remington GAIL since key assignments are virtually identical, with Unicode character output.

---

## 2. Speed Evaluation & Practice Recommendations

* Focus on typing administrative circulars, reports, and scheme documents commonly used in rural development departments.
* Ensure a consistent typing rhythm: aim for 35 WPM in English and 30 WPM in Hindi during practice to secure a comfortable safety buffer on exam day.
