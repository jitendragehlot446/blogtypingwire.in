The **Delhi Development Authority (DDA)** conducts recruitment for **Junior Secretariat Assistant (JSA)** and **Assistant Section Officer (ASO)**.

As part of the **Stage-II evaluation**, candidates shortlisted from the Stage-I computer-based examination must appear for the mandatory **Computer Typing Test**. Below are the official guidelines, speed benchmarks, and evaluation rules.

---

## ⚡ Quick Exam Snapshot (TL;DR Fact Matrix)

| Parameter | English Typing Medium | Hindi Typing Medium |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Minimum Required Speed** | **35 Words Per Minute (WPM)** | **30 Words Per Minute (WPM)** |
| **Key Depressions (KDPH)** | **10,500 KDPH on Computer** | **9,000 KDPH on Computer** |
| **Test Duration** | **10 Minutes** | **10 Minutes** |
| **Required Keystrokes** | **1,750 Keystrokes (±5%)** | **1,500 Keystrokes (±5%)** |
| **Font Standard** | Standard English Font | Mangal Unicode (Inscript / Remington Gail) |
| **Software Platform** | TCS iON / Central Testing Platform | TCS iON / Central Testing Platform |
| **Test Nature** | **Qualifying (Stage-II)** | **Qualifying (Stage-II)** |
| **Backspace Permitted** | **Yes (Full Editing Enabled)** | **Yes (Full Editing Enabled)** |

---

## 1. Speed Calculation & Official Formula

The evaluation follows central government norms:
* **Standard Word**: 5 Keystrokes = 1 Word.
* **Gross Speed (WPM)**:
  `Gross Speed = (Total Key Depressions Typed / 5 / 10 Minutes)`
* **Net Speed (WPM)**:
  `Net Speed = Gross Speed - ((Mistakes Penalty / 10))`

Candidates must achieve a minimum Net Speed of **35 WPM** in English or **30 WPM** in Hindi to be declared qualified.

---

## 2. TCS iON Software Interface & Examination Hall Tips

* **Split-Screen Layout**: The software displays the master passage in the upper pane and your input in the lower pane.
* **No Auto-Correction**: Spellings are not underlined in red or auto-capitalized. Candidates must type precisely according to the master text.
* **Scrolling**: The passage spans multiple paragraphs. Candidates must use the mouse scroll wheel or page-down keys to access remaining lines.
