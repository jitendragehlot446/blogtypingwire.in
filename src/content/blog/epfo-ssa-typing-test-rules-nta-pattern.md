The **Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO)** recruits thousands of candidates for the post of **Social Security Assistant (SSA) - Group 'C'** through the **National Testing Agency (NTA)**.

Candidates qualifying Phase-I are called for the **Phase-II Computer Data Entry / Typing Skill Test**.

## 1. NTA Typing Test Interface & Software Behavior

1. **Split-Screen Layout**: Master passage is situated on the upper half; candidate input textarea is situated on the lower half.
2. **Current Word Highlighting**: The active target word is highlighted with background contrast.
3. **Auto-Scrolling**: Passage scrolls automatically to reveal subsequent lines.
4. **Infinite Backspace**: Candidates can backspace freely within words and navigate across typed lines using arrow keys.

---

## 2. Speed & Error Calculation Algorithm

`Standard Words Typed = (Total Key Depressions Typed / 5)`

`Net Speed (WPM) = (Standard Words - Mistakes Penalty / 10 Minutes)`

* Candidates must maintain **at least 93% to 95% net accuracy** to ensure their net speed remains above the 35 WPM qualifying line.
