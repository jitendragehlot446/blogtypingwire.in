The **Council of Scientific and Industrial Research (CSIR)** recruits **Junior Secretariat Assistants (JSA - General, Stores & Purchase, and Finance & Accounts)** across its nationwide network of premier national research laboratories and headquarters.

Candidates qualifying Paper-I and Paper-II written examinations must clear the mandatory **Computer Typing Test**. Below are the official rules, speed criteria, and guidelines.

---

## ⚡ Quick Exam Snapshot (TL;DR Fact Matrix)

| Parameter | English Typing Standard | Hindi Typing Standard |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Minimum Required Speed** | **35 Words Per Minute (WPM)** | **30 Words Per Minute (WPM)** |
| **Key Depressions (KDPH)** | **10,500 KDPH on Computer** | **9,000 KDPH on Computer** |
| **Test Duration** | **10 Minutes** | **10 Minutes** |
| **Passage Volume** | **Approx. 350 Words (1,750 Keystrokes)** | **Approx. 300 Words (1,500 Keystrokes)** |
| **Font Standard** | Standard English Font | Mangal Unicode (Inscript / Remington GAIL) |
| **Test Nature** | **Qualifying Only (Mandatory)** | **Qualifying Only (Mandatory)** |
| **Backspace Permitted** | **Yes (Full Editing Enabled)** | **Yes (Full Editing Enabled)** |

---

## 1. Official Speed Calculation Formula

* **Standard Character Count**: 5 key depressions = 1 word.
* **Speed Formula**:
  `Net Speed (WPM) = (Correct Keystrokes / 5 / 10 Minutes)`
* Accuracy is critical: Uncorrected spelling mistakes, missing words, and incorrect capitalization result in direct word penalties that reduce net speed.

---

## 2. Examination Hall Preparation Strategy

* **Scientific & Administrative Terminology**: Passages may include institutional correspondence, reports, and administrative forms.
* **Typing Stamina**: Maintain an even typing speed throughout the 10-minute session. Practice with standard keyboards rather than low-profile laptop keyboards.
