The **Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC)** conducts computer-based typing tests for **Junior Assistant, Junior Clerk, and Assistant Grade-III** recruitment.

---

## 1. Exam Structure & Speed Criteria

| Paper | Language | Duration | Key Depressions Required | Required Net Speed |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Paper-I** | **Hindi Typing** (Mangal/KrutiDev) | **5 Minutes** | 625 Keystrokes (125 Words) | **25 WPM** |
| **Paper-II** | **English Typing** | **5 Minutes** | 750 Keystrokes (150 Words) | **30 WPM** |

*Note: Qualifying in BOTH languages is mandatory.*

---

## 2. UPSSSC Mistake Deduction & Penalty Formula

* **Errors 0 to 5**: Completely exempt (100% forgiven).
* **Errors beyond 5**: For every single error exceeding 5, **5 words penalty** is deducted.

$$\text{Excess Errors} = \text{Total Mistakes} - 5 \quad (\text{if Total Mistakes} > 5)$$

$$\text{Net Speed (WPM)} = \frac{\text{Correct Words Typed} - (\text{Excess Errors} \times 5)}{5 \text{ Minutes}}$$
