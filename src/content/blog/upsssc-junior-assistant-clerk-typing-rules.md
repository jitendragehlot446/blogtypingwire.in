The **Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC)** conducts recruitment for thousands of **Junior Assistant (कनिष्ठ सहायक), Junior Clerk, and Assistant Grade-III** vacancies across state government departments.

UPSSSC is unique among state exams because **passing BOTH Hindi (25 WPM) AND English (30 WPM) typing is strictly compulsory**.

## 1. Hindi Font Choice: Mangal vs KrutiDev 010

UPSSSC provides candidates the flexibility to choose their preferred Hindi typing system at the test terminal:
* **Option A: KrutiDev 010 / DevLys 010**: Remington typewriter layout on legacy ASCII font.
* **Option B: Mangal Inscript**: Standard Unicode layout.
* **Option C: Mangal Remington (Gail)**: Typewriter layout mapped to Unicode script.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> If a candidate types at 60 WPM in English but achieves 24.8 WPM in Hindi, they are declared **FAILED in the entire recruitment**. Never neglect your weaker typing language!

---

## 2. Speed Evaluation Formula (5-Minute Short Sprint)

Because the test is only 5 minutes long, even a minor freeze or keyboard jam can be disastrous:

`Gross Words = (Total Keystrokes Typed / 5)`

`Net Speed (WPM) = (Gross Words - Mistakes Penalty / Time (5 Minutes))`

* In a 5-minute test, every second counts. Candidates must type with immediate fluidity from second zero.
