The **High Court of Judicature at Patna (Patna High Court)** conducts rigorous computer skill tests for recruitment to **Assistant (Group-B), Stenographer (Group-C), and Court Reader** cadres. 

Unlike central qualifying examinations, Patna High Court requires a high benchmark of **40 WPM with 90% accuracy**, along with a practical **Computer Proficiency Test (CPT)**.

## 1. Speed & Accuracy Standards Breakdown

### 🅰️ English Typing Test (Assistant & Court Reader)
* **Duration**: 10 Minutes.
* **Target Word Count**: Candidates must type approximately 400 words without excessive errors.
* **Accuracy Requirement**: Minimum **90% Net Accuracy**. If your gross speed is 45 WPM but error rate exceeds 10%, you are disqualified immediately.
* **Formula**:
`Net Speed (WPM) = (Correct Words Typed - Penalty Words / Time in Minutes (10))`

### 🅱️ English & Hindi Stenography Standards
* **Dictation Duration**: 5 minutes at continuous **80 words per minute** (400 words total).
* **Reading / Review Time**: 10 minutes to review shorthand notes.
* **Computer Transcription**: 30 minutes to type the transcribed passage on the computer terminal.
* **Permissible Mistakes**: Maximum **5% errors allowed** (95% accuracy required) for General candidates.

## 2. Computer Proficiency Test (CPT) Syllabus

The CPT evaluates practical workplace computing skills in three specific modules:

### 1. MS Word (Formatting & Drafting)
* Page layout, margin setup (Narrow, Moderate, 1-inch standard).
* Paragraph indents, line spacing (1.15, 1.5, double), and justification (`Ctrl + J`).
* Text effects: Bold, Italic, Underline, Superscript/Subscript, Strikethrough.
* Insertion of tables, header, footer, page numbers, and official legal headings.

### 2. MS Excel (Data Processing & Calculations)
* Spreadsheet formulas: `=SUM()`, `=AVERAGE()`, `=COUNT()`, `=IF()`, `=MAX()`, `=MIN()`.
* Data sorting, cell merging, currency/percentage number formatting.
* Table borders and simple bar/column chart generation.

### 3. Internet & Workplace Communication
* Basic web search operations, downloading attachments, and drafting formal emails.

## 3. High-Scoring Preparation Tips for Patna High Court

1. **Target 48+ WPM in Daily Practice**: Because exam pressure and unfamiliar mechanical keyboards reduce typing speed by 5–8 WPM, maintaining a consistent home practice speed of 48 WPM guarantees clearing the 40 WPM exam threshold.
2. **Train on Legal Vocabulary**: Patna High Court passages heavily feature judicial terms like *appellant, affidavit, petitioner, jurisprudence, decree, jurisdiction, respondent*. Practice with legal passages directly on [TypingWire.in](https://typingwire.in).
3. **Control Backspace Addiction**: Frequent backspacing shatters rhythm. Aim for **96%+ raw accuracy** before pushing for raw speed.
4. **Master MS Word Keyboard Shortcuts**: Knowing shortcuts (`Ctrl + E` center, `Ctrl + B` bold, `Ctrl + 1` single line spacing) saves 3–4 valuable minutes in the CPT module.
