The **Patna High Court (PHC)** Assistant (Group-B) and Stenographer recruitment includes a computer-based Typing Test followed by a Computer Proficiency Test (CPT).

---

## 1. Speed & Accuracy Standards

| Component | Minimum Speed | Minimum Accuracy Required | Duration |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **English Typing** | **40 WPM** | **90% Accuracy** | 10 Minutes |
| **Hindi Typing** | **30 WPM** | **85% Accuracy** | 10 Minutes |
| **Steno Dictation** | **80 WPM** | **95% Accuracy** | 5 Min Dictation + 30 Min Transcription |

---

## 2. Computer Proficiency Test (CPT) Modules
1. **MS Word**: Formatting official letters, legal drafts, indents, margins.
2. **MS Excel**: Basic formulas (`SUM`, `AVERAGE`, `IF`), cell formatting, charts.
3. **Internet & Emailing**: Web research, email drafting, attachment management.
