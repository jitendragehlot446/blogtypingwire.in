Aspirants preparing for Staff Selection Commission exams often ask whether practicing for **SSC CGL DEST** is sufficient to clear **SSC CHSL Typing Test**, or vice versa.

While both examinations use the same TCS iON testing platform, their speed thresholds, time limits, and error tolerance cutoffs differ significantly.

---

## ⚡ Direct Comparison: SSC CGL DEST vs SSC CHSL

| Evaluation Parameter | SSC CGL DEST (Tier-II) | SSC CHSL Typing Test (Tier-II) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Applicable Posts** | All CGL Posts (ASO, Tax Assistant, Inspector) | LDC, JSA, DEO (Data Entry Operator) |
| **Duration of Test** | **15 Minutes** | **10 Minutes** (15 mins for DEO Grade A) |
| **Target Keystrokes** | **2,000 Key Depressions (±5%)** | **1,750 Key Depressions (English LDC)** |
| **Effective Net Speed** | **~27 Words Per Minute (WPM)** | **35 WPM (English) / 30 WPM (Hindi)** |
| **Error Limit (UR / Gen)**| **5% for High-Cutoff Posts / 20% Standard**| **7% Maximum Errors Allowed** |
| **Error Limit (Reserved)**| **7% for High-Cutoff Posts / 25% Standard**| **10% Maximum Errors Allowed** |
| **DEO Grade A Stream** | N/A | **15,000 KDPH (~50 WPM) in 15 Minutes** |
| **Conducting Software** | TCS iON Split-Screen Browser | TCS iON Split-Screen Browser |
| **Backspace Permitted** | **Yes (Full Editing Enabled)** | **Yes (Full Editing Enabled)** |
| **Nature of Paper** | Mandatory Qualifying | Mandatory Qualifying |

## 1. Key Speed & Duration Differences Explained

### 1. The Endurance Factor (15 Mins vs 10 Mins)
* **SSC CGL DEST (15 Minutes)**: Requires sustained stamina over a longer period. Finger fatigue typically begins around minute 10 to 12. Maintaining a calm pace of 30–32 WPM ensures finishing 2,000 keystrokes with 4 minutes left to proofread.
* **SSC CHSL (10 Minutes)**: A rapid sprint requiring **35 WPM from the start**. Typists cannot afford a sluggish start or excessive pauses.
