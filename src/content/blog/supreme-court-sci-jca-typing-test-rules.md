The **Supreme Court of India (SCI)** conducts one of the strictest typing examinations for the post of **Junior Court Assistant (JCA)**.

---

## 1. Exam Specifications & Speed Standards

| Parameter | Specification |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Medium** | English (Mandatory) |
| **Duration** | 10 Minutes |
| **Target Key Depressions** | 1,750 to 2,100 Keystrokes |
| **Minimum Qualifying Speed** | **35 WPM** |
| **Permissible Error Limit** | **Maximum 3% Mistakes** |
| **Backspace** | Allowed (Active Word) |

---

## 2. Evaluation & Penalty Formula
$$\text{Net Speed (WPM)} = \frac{\text{Correct Words Typed} - (\text{Mistakes} \times \text{Penalty})}{\text{10 Minutes}}$$
