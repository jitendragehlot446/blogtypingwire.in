The **Staff Selection Commission (SSC)** allows candidates appearing for **SSC CGL, CHSL, Selection Post, and Departmental Examinations** to take their typing test in either English or Hindi.

For Hindi typists, SSC provides multiple keyboard layout options on **Unicode Mangal font**, making it essential to understand which layout suits your training background.

## 1. Comparing the 3 SSC Hindi Keyboard Layouts

### 1. Mangal Remington (Gail) — *Most Popular*
* **Design Philosophy**: Replicates the mechanical Remington Hindi typewriter layout mapped directly into modern Unicode characters.
* **Best For**: Candidates who learned typing in private computer typing institutes or traditional typewriter coaching centers.
* **Halant & Half Letters**: Typing half letters requires standard keys (`D` key acts as Halant).

### 2. Mangal Inscript — *Official Government of India Standard*
* **Design Philosophy**: Standardized by the Department of Electronics. Left side houses vowels, right side houses consonants.
* **Best For**: Candidates comfortable with touch-typing systems built natively into Windows, macOS, and Linux.

### 3. Remington (CBI) — *Central Bureau of Investigation Layout*
* **Design Philosophy**: A minor variant of Remington Gail originally standardized for central investigating agencies, differing primarily in punctuation and inverted comma assignments.

## 2. Common Hindi Typing Traps in SSC Exams

1. **The 'Chhoti I' Matra (ि)**: On modern Unicode Remington Gail, you must type the consonant first, then the matra key. Typing the matra first causes input errors.
2. **Nukta Characters (क़, ख़, ग़, ज़, फ़)**: Nukta letters frequently appear in SSC administrative passages. Ensure you know the Nukta shortcut key.
3. **Punctuation (पूर्णविराम |)**: In Remington Gail, `Shift + A` produces the Devanagari full stop (*|*).
