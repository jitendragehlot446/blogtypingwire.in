Candidates opting for the **Hindi Medium** in **SSC CGL DEST, SSC CHSL, or SSC Stenographer** tests can choose from three officially recognized keyboard layouts on the **Unicode Mangal Font**.

---

## 1. The 3 Official SSC Hindi Layouts

| Layout Name | Typing Style | Recommended For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hindi Inscript** | Standard Government Layout (Vowels left, Consonants right) | New learners and candidates using modern Windows keyboards. |
| **Hindi Remington (GAIL)** | Traditional Mechanical Typewriter Mapping | Candidates trained on Remington typewriter keys. |
| **Hindi Remington (CBI)** | Typewriter Variant (Alternative conjunct mapping) | Candidates trained specifically in Central Bureau/CBI stenography schools. |

---

## 2. Important Characters & Halant Usage in Mangal
* On **Inscript**, half-letters and conjuncts (e.g. `क्त`, `स्थ`, `द्वा`) are generated by typing the consonant followed by `D` (Halant) and the next consonant.
* On **Remington Gail**, full letters with Shift/unShift keys mimic legacy typewriter mechanics.
