## 1. Beltron DEO Speed & Accuracy Formula

Beltron evaluates candidate typing through an automated algorithm:

`Gross Speed (WPM) = (Total Keystrokes Typed / 5 / Time (10 Minutes))`

`Net Speed (WPM) = Gross Speed - ( (Total Mistakes / Time (10 Minutes)) )`

`Accuracy Percentage = ( (Correct Words Typed / Total Words Typed) ) × 100`

> [!IMPORTANT]
> To qualify for Beltron empanelment, your **Net Speed must be \ge 30 WPM in Hindi** AND your **Accuracy must be \ge 90%**. High gross speed with sloppy accuracy will cause immediate failure.

## 2. Common Hindi Typing Mistakes on Mangal Remington Gail

1. **Incorrect Matra Sequences**: In Remington Gail, typing the Chhoti 'I' (*इ*) matra requires typing the consonant first, unlike manual typewriters where matra was typed before the letter.
2. **Missing Halant on Conjuncts**: Complex characters like *प्र, क्र, त्र, श्र, द्ध* must be typed using standard Remington key positions (`Shift + 3`, `D`, etc.).
3. **Space Bar Before Punctuation**: Inserting an accidental space before full stop (*पूर्णविराम |*) or comma counts as an error.
