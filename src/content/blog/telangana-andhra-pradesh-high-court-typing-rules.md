## 1. Speed Benchmark & SBTET Certification Requirements

Unlike exams requiring 30–35 WPM, Telangana and AP High Court mandate the **Higher Grade (45 WPM)** technical standard:
* **Government Technical Examination (GTE)** in Typewriting by Higher Grade in English conducted by SBTET Andhra Pradesh / Telangana is an essential eligibility prerequisite.
* Passages consist of high-density judicial orders, statutory references, and Latin legal maxims (*prima facie, habeas corpus, mandamus, sub judice*).

---

## 2. Evaluation Formula & Deduction Rules

Examiners calculate mistakes based on word-level integrity:
1. **Omission / Addition Penalty**: Every omitted word deducts **1 full mark / word equivalent**.
2. **Spelling & Punctuation Errors**: Every misspelled word or misplaced punctuation mark deducts **0.5 mark**.
3. **Speed Cutoff Threshold**: Candidates typing below 45 Net WPM are disqualified instantly regardless of high written scores.

`Net Speed (WPM) = (Total Keystrokes Typed / 5 - Mistakes Penalty / Time (10 Minutes))`

1. **Aim for 55+ WPM in Practice**: To comfortably maintain 45 WPM under exam conditions, build your raw practice speed to at least 55 WPM.
2. **Master Legal Nomenclature**: Practice reading and typing complex judicial judgments on [TypingWire.in](https://typingwire.in).
