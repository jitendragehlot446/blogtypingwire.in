The **Centralized Selection and Appointment Committee (Patna District Court)** conducts recruitment for the District Courts of Bihar, covering **Clerk, Court Reader-cum-Deposition Writer, and Stenographer**. Similarly, the **Bihar Legislative Assembly (Vidhan Sabha)** and **Bihar Legislative Council (Vidhan Parishad)** conduct administrative typing evaluations.

## 1. Bihar Civil Court Court Reader (CRDW) Evaluation Scheme

Candidates for the Court Reader post must score qualifying marks in both typing sessions:

### 📑 Part-A: English Typing Test
* **Duration**: 10 Minutes.
* **Prescribed Speed**: **40 Words Per Minute (WPM)**.
* **Total Words**: Approximately 400 words.
* **Accuracy Threshold**: Minimum **90% accuracy**. A maximum of 10% errors are tolerated; any higher error percentage results in straight rejection.

### 📑 Part-B: Hindi Typing Test
* **Duration**: 10 Minutes.
* **Prescribed Speed**: **30 Words Per Minute (WPM)**.
* **Total Words**: Approximately 300 words.
* **Font & Layout**: **Mangal Unicode font** on **Remington (Gail) or Inscript** keyboard layout.
* **Accuracy Threshold**: Minimum **90% accuracy**.

## 2. Mistake Calculation & Deduction Methodology

Errors in the Bihar Civil Court and Vidhan Sabha typing tests are categorized into full mistakes and formatting penalties:

### 🔴 Full Mistakes (Full Word Deduction)
* **Omission of Word**: Missing words from the master court deposition text.
* **Substitution of Word**: Typing an entirely incorrect word instead of the original word.
* **Addition / Insertion**: Adding unprinted words or phrases into the text.

### 🟡 Minor Mistakes (Spelling & Spacing)
* **Spelling Inaccuracies**: Transposed letters (e.g., typing *tset* instead of *test*).
* **Missing Punctuation**: Skipping periods, commas, or quotation marks.
* **Case Mismatch**: Lowercase letters typed where capitalized letters were required.

$$\text{Net Speed (WPM)} = \frac{\text{Total Words Typed} - (\text{Mistakes} \times 5)}{\text{Time in Minutes (10)}}$$
