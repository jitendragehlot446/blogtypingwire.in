The **Bombay High Court (BHC)** conducts a strict Computer Typing Test for the recruitment of **Clerk, Typist, Peon/Hamal, and Stenographer (Grade-III)** posts.

---

## 1. Exam Structure & Speed Criteria

| Post / Subject | Speed Required | Duration | Total Marks | Minimum Cutoff |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **English Typing** | **40 WPM** | 10 Minutes | 20 Marks | Minimum 10 Marks |
| **Marathi Typing** (Optional/Post-Specific) | **30 WPM** | 10 Minutes | 20 Marks | Minimum 10 Marks |

---

## 2. Evaluation & Penalty Rules
* **Omissions & Additions**: Every missed word carries a proportional penalty.
* **Accuracy Requirement**: A minimum accuracy of **95%** is strongly recommended to secure competitive merit marks.
