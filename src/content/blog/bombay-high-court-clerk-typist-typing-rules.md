The **High Court of Judicature at Bombay (Bombay High Court)** conducts typing and computer skill tests for recruitment to **Clerk, Typist, and Stenographer (Lower / Higher Grade)** across its principal seat at Mumbai and benches at Nagpur, Aurangabad, and Goa.

## 1. Exam Scheme & Marking Methodology

The skill test assesses the candidate's speed, linguistic accuracy, and transcription precision:

### 📑 Paper-I: English Typing Test (20 Marks)
* Candidates are given a printed/screen passage containing judicial judgments, petitions, and legal drafting text.
* Total Duration: **10 minutes**.
* **Formula for Mark Deduction**: Every spelling mistake, missed word, or punctuation omission deducts **0.25 to 0.5 marks** from the base 20 marks.

### 📑 Paper-II: Marathi Typing Test (20 Marks)
* Conducted on standard Unicode Marathi keyboards (Mangal font).
* Evaluates complex conjunct letters (*जोडाक्षरे*), halant formations, and matras.
* Total Duration: **10 minutes**.

---

## 2. Mistake Classification in Bombay High Court

1. **Gross Mistakes**: Missing complete lines, skipping words, substituting words.
2. **Minor Mistakes**: Punctuation mistakes, incorrect space before/after comma, capitalization errors.
3. **Incomplete Submissions**: Candidates who type less than the requisite words receive proportionate deductions and fail to meet the qualifying 10-mark cutoff.

## 3. Preparation Strategy for Bombay High Court Aspirants

1. **Practice Both Marathi & English Regularly**: Allocate 30 minutes daily to Marathi typing on Mangal Inscript or Remington layout to master complex Marathi ligatures.
2. **Use Legal Terminology Passages**: High Court passages frequently incorporate terms like *writ petition, interlocutory application, interim order, caveat, maintainability*.
