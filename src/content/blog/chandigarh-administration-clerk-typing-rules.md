The **Chandigarh Administration** recruits **Clerks and Steno-Typists** across departments, secretariats, and municipal corporations in the Union Territory of Chandigarh.

Following the written examination, candidates must clear the computer typing test. Below are the official rules, speed criteria, and mistake evaluation guidelines.

---

| Parameter | Chandigarh Clerk Official Standard |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Recruitment Post** | Clerk / Steno-Typist (Group C) |
| **Mandatory Medium** | **English on Computer** |
| **Minimum Required Speed** | **30 Words Per Minute (WPM)** |
| **Keystrokes Equivalent** | **1,500 Correct Keystrokes in 10 Minutes** |

| **Permissible Error Limit** | **8% Maximum Errors** |
| **Passage Length** | **Approx. 300–350 Words** |

| **Backspace Usage** | **Allowed (Full Editing)** |

## 1. Official Evaluation Formula

* **Standard Calculation**: 1 Word = 5 Keystrokes.
* **Calculation Algorithm**:
  $$\text{Net Speed (WPM)} = \frac{\text{Total Keystrokes Typed} - (\text{Penalty for Errors} \times 5)}{5 \times 10 \text{ Minutes}}$$
* Candidates who type the full 300 words within 10 minutes with an accuracy of 92% or higher comfortably clear the official 30 WPM threshold.

---

## 2. Test Interface & Candidate Tips

* **Desktop Environment**: Typing tests are held in local Chandigarh testing institutes (such as SPIC, NIELIT, or university computer labs).
* **Maintain Rhythm**: Since 30 WPM is a moderate speed, avoid rushing during the first 2 minutes. Typing steadily at 35 WPM ensures complete coverage with zero fatigue.
