The **High Court of Himachal Pradesh (Shimla)** conducts recruitment for **Clerk, Proof Reader, Junior Office Assistant (IT), and Stenographer** across the High Court registry and subordinate district courts of Himachal Pradesh.

## 1. Speed & Mistake Calculation Formula

`Gross Words = (Total Key Depressions Typed / 5)`

`Error Percentage (%) = ( (Total Mistakes / Total Words Typed) ) × 100`

`Net Speed (WPM) = (Gross Words - Mistakes Penalty / Time (10 Minutes))`
