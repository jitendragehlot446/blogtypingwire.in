The **Society for Centralized Recruitment of Staff in Subordinate Courts (S.S.S.C.)** on behalf of the **High Court of Punjab and Haryana at Chandigarh** recruits thousands of candidates for the post of **Clerk** across the Subordinate Courts of Punjab, Haryana, and U.T. Chandigarh.

The selection process includes a Written CBT followed by the mandatory **Stage-II Computer Proficiency Test (CPT)** comprising an **English Typing Test (30 WPM)** and a **Spreadsheet Test**.

## 1. SSSC Typing Speed & Error Calculation Algorithm

SSSC evaluates candidate transcripts based on exact net speed:

`Gross Words = (Total Key Depressions Typed / 5)`

`Percentage Errors (%) = ( (Total Mistakes Committed / Total Words Typed) ) × 100`

`Net Speed (WPM) = (Gross Words - Mistakes Penalty / 10 Minutes)`

> [!IMPORTANT]
> To qualify, candidates must satisfy **BOTH conditions**:
> 1. Net speed must be **\ge 30.0 WPM**.
> 2. Total mistakes must **not exceed 8.0%** of the words typed.

## 2. Part-II: 10-Mark Spreadsheet Test Syllabus (MS Excel)

Candidates qualifying the speed test immediately take the 10-minute Excel test:
1. **Mathematical Operations**: Applying `=SUM()`, `=AVERAGE()`, subtraction, and division formulas across rows and columns.
2. **Cell & Table Formatting**: Merging cells for headings, applying all-around borders, setting column widths, aligning numbers to the right.
3. **Data Types**: Formatting numeric values with currency symbols (₹) or standard two decimal places (`0.00`).
* **Minimum Qualifying Cutoff**: **3.3 out of 10 marks**.
