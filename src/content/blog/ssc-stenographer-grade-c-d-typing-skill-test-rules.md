The **SSC Stenographer Grade 'C' & 'D' Examination** includes a practical **Skill Test in Stenography**. Candidates take dictation in shorthand and transcribe the notes onto a computer within a strict time limit.

---

## 1. Dictation Speed & Transcription Time

| Grade | Medium | Dictation Speed | Dictation Time | Total Words | Computer Transcription Time |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Grade 'C'** | English | **100 WPM** | 10 Minutes | 1,000 Words | **40 Minutes** |
| **Grade 'C'** | Hindi | **100 WPM** | 10 Minutes | 1,000 Words | **55 Minutes** |
| **Grade 'D'** | English | **80 WPM** | 10 Minutes | 800 Words | **50 Minutes** |
| **Grade 'D'** | Hindi | **80 WPM** | 10 Minutes | 800 Words | **65 Minutes** |

---

## 2. Category-Wise Permissible Error Percentage

| Category | Permissible Error (Grade 'C') | Permissible Error (Grade 'D') |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **UR (General)** | **5% (Max 50 Mistakes)** | **7% (Max 56 Mistakes)** |
| **OBC / EWS** | **7% (Max 70 Mistakes)** | **10% (Max 80 Mistakes)** |
| **SC / ST** | **7% (Max 70 Mistakes)** | **10% (Max 80 Mistakes)** |
| **PwD (OH/VH/HH)** | **10% (Max 100 Mistakes)** | **10% (Max 80 Mistakes)** |

---

## 3. Shorthand Transcription Evaluation: Full vs Half Mistakes

### 🔴 Full Mistakes (100% Deduction)
* **Every omission** of a word or figure from the master dictation.
* **Every substitution** of a wrong word or figure.
* **Every addition** of a word or figure not present in the master dictation.

### 🟡 Half Mistakes (50% Deduction)
* **Spelling errors** or incorrect singular/plural forms.
* **Omission of punctuation marks** (full stop, comma, question mark).
* **Capitalization errors** (small letter for proper nouns like names of countries, persons).
